# 8 AI prompts to optimize your Zoom presence

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Productivity** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/8-ai-prompts-to-optimize-your-zoom-presence/26213384788267674/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The Audio-Visual Audit

```text
Act as a Professional AV Technician and Videographer. I want to audit my current video conferencing setup to look and sound more professional. Here is my current gear list: [Insert Webcam/Camera Model], [Insert Microphone Model], and [Insert Computer Specs]. My room environment is [Describe Room Size and Surface Types like carpet or hardwood]. Analyze this setup and tell me the single biggest bottleneck preventing high-quality video or audio, and provide a fix that costs less than $50. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. The "Cinematic" Lighting Fix

```text
Act as a Professional Lighting Director for Film and TV. I need to look better on Zoom calls, but my lighting is flat or unflattering. Currently, my light sources are [Describe Windows/Lamps and their position relative to you]. My desk faces [Insert Direction/Wall or Window]. Give me step-by-step instructions on where to position myself and my lights to achieve a "Rembrandt" or professional 3-point lighting look using only what I have or cheap household items. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. The Executive Background Designer

```text
Act as a Set Designer and Brand Consultant. I want my Zoom background to look professional and align with my personal brand. I work in the [Insert Industry] industry and want to convey a vibe of [Insert Desired Vibe, e.g., Creativity, Trust, Authority]. The space behind me currently has [Describe Bookshelves, Blank Wall, or Clutter]. Give me 3 distinct layout ideas for my background that I can set up this weekend to impress clients. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. The Audio Engineer Equalizer

```text
Act as a Professional Audio Engineer. I am conducting meetings in a room that sounds [Describe Audio Issues, e.g., Echoey, Noisy, or Hollow]. I am using [Insert Software/Hardware, e.g., Zoom standard audio or a specific mic]. Please give me a checklist of software settings in Zoom and physical changes I can make to the room (like placing rugs or curtains) to dampen the sound and make my voice sound broadcast quality. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. The Confidence Monitor Setup

```text
Act as a Communication and Public Speaking Coach. I struggle with looking at the camera versus looking at the participants on my screen during [Insert Meeting Type, e.g., Sales Calls or Team Updates]. My current monitor setup is [Describe Monitor Count and Position]. Design a screen layout workflow for me that ensures I maintain simulated eye contact while still being able to read my notes and see the other person's reactions. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. The Connection Stabilizer

```text
Act as a Network Administrator. I experience occasional lag or quality drops on Zoom. My internet speed is roughly [Insert Upload/Download Speed] and I connect via [Wifi or Ethernet]. Other devices on the network include [List Other Devices like Smart TVs or Phones]. Provide a troubleshooting checklist to prioritize my computer's traffic and optimize my Zoom settings for stability over resolution. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 7. The Budget Upgrade Roadmap

```text
Act as a Tech Procurement Specialist. I have a budget of [Insert Budget Amount] to upgrade my Zoom setup. I want the highest impact on quality possible. Based on my current setup of [Insert Current Webcam and Mic], tell me exactly what item I should buy first, second, and third to maximize the professional look and feel of my calls. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 8. The 2-Minute Pre-Call Checklist

```text
Act as an Operations Manager. I need a fool-proof pre-flight checklist to run 5 minutes before every high-stakes client call. Create a concise, 7-point checklist that covers audio, video, lighting, internet, and background checks to ensure [Insert Your Job Title] level professionalism every time I join a meeting. Ask me any questions you have.
```
