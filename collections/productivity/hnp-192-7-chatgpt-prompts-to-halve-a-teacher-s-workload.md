# 7 ChatGPT Prompts to Halve a Teacher's Workload

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Productivity & Life Admin** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/100089314351644/posts/teachers-these-7-chatgpt-prompts-will-halve-your-workload1-design-a-40-minute-le/972716219048848/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Lesson plan

```text
Design a 40-minute lesson plan on [topic] for [grade level], including objectives, warm-up, main activity, and closing review.
```

### 2. Student summary

```text
Summarize this [chapter/article] into a student-ready summary for [grade level].
```

### 3. Quiz questions

```text
Write 12 quiz questions (multiple choice + short answer) on [topic] with an answer key.
```

### 4. Creative activities

```text
Suggest 5 creative activities to teach [concept] to [age group].
```

### 5. Parent update

```text
Draft a weekly parent update explaining what students learned in [subject], written in a positive tone.
```

### 6. Differentiated assignments

```text
Develop assignments on [topic] at beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels.
```

### 7. Grading rubric

```text
Build a grading rubric for [assignment type] that's clear, balanced, and fair.
```
