# 5 prompts to unlock ChatGPT 5's full potential

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Productivity & Life Admin** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/Owwstin/posts/chatgpt-5-is-insanely-powerful-and-free-but-almost-no-one-is-using-it-to-its-ful/10160983771666256/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The Hyper-Specialist Blueprint

```text
You are ChatGPT 5 running the most advanced reasoning mode. Pretend you are a [specific expert] with 20+ years of experience. I'll give you a goal — break it into every micro-step in logical order, then expand each step into a full playbook with scripts, templates, and recommended tools. Anticipate mistakes and add fixes.
```

### 2. The Perfection Loop

```text
I will paste my [text/plan/script]. As ChatGPT 5, refine it in rounds. For each round, produce 3 improved variations with an explanation of what changed and why. Ask 3 precision questions before the next round to sharpen the result. Keep going until I say 'stop.'
```

### 3. The Results Reverse-Engineer

```text
As ChatGPT 5 with multi-domain expertise, pretend you just achieved [specific impressive result]. Reverse-engineer the exact process: every decision, resource, and tool, in the precise order you used them, with time estimates and risk warnings.
```

### 4. The Deep-Dive Synthesizer

```text
Using your GPT-5 reasoning capabilities, pull from the most accurate knowledge you have up to today. Find the 20 most relevant sources about [topic], summarize each, then synthesize a single 'master guide' that removes contradictions, fills in gaps, and organizes the content into a step-by-step system.
```

### 5. The Multi-Layer Context Builder

```text
You are ChatGPT 5. I'll give you 3 layers of context: audience, goal, and constraints. Use all layers to produce output that's not just accurate but feels made for me. At the end, explain exactly how each layer shaped your response.
```
