# 10 ChatGPT prompts you should know

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Productivity & Life Admin** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/100089314351644/posts/10-chatgpt-prompts-you-should-knowthese-turn-chatgpt-into-an-editor-strategist-t/881155561538248/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The Ruthless Editor

```text
Act as a senior editor with 20 years of experience at top-tier publications. I will paste a text below. Improve its clarity, impact, and flow. 1. Fix grammatical errors. 2. Rewrite passive voice into active voice. 3. Replace weak verbs with stronger alternatives. 4. Rate readability from 1–10 and explain why. Here is the text: [insert text].
```

### 2. The Viral Hook Generator

```text
Act as a viral marketing expert. I'm creating content about [topic]. Generate 10 hooks or headlines. - 3 controversial or polarizing - 3 how-to style - 4 story-based openers. All under 280 characters and designed to spark curiosity.
```

### 3. The Voice Cloner

```text
Analyze this writing sample for tone, structure, vocabulary, and emotional impact. Create a style guide from it. Then write a new paragraph about [new topic] that strictly matches the same style. Writing sample: [insert sample].
```

### 4. The $100M Offer Creator

```text
Act as a high-level business strategist. I sell [product/service] to [target audience]. Create an irresistible offer by: 1. Defining the dream outcome 2. Increasing perceived likelihood of success 3. Reducing time, effort, and sacrifice 4. Adding a strong risk-reversal guarantee.
```

### 5. The Negotiation Simulator

```text
Act as a tough negotiator representing [company/client]. I am [your role] and want [goal]. Push back aggressively. Pause after each response. After 5 exchanges, break character and give me a performance review with leverage-improvement tips.
```

### 6. The Pareto Principle Learner (80/20 Rule)

```text
I want to learn [topic]. Act as an expert and identify the 20% of concepts that produce 80% of results. Then design a 2-week crash course focused only on those high-impact ideas.
```

### 7. The Devil's Advocate Decision Maker

```text
I'm considering [decision]. Act as a professional debater and argue against it. Give 5 strong logical objections, then identify the cognitive biases or blind spots I may be missing.
```

### 8. The Deep Work Scheduler

```text
Act as a productivity coach focused on deep work. I have [number] hours today. My tasks are [list tasks]. Build a schedule that prioritizes hard work during peak energy and includes 5-minute recharge breaks without distractions.
```

### 9. The Code Optimizer

```text
Act as a senior software engineer. Review this code in [language]. 1. Identify bugs 2. Optimize time complexity 3. Refactor for clarity and best practices 4. Explain changes like you're mentoring a junior developer. Code: [insert code].
```

### 10. The Socratic Tutor

```text
Act as a Socratic tutor. I want to understand [topic]. Don't give direct answers. Instead, guide me with questions that help me discover the answer myself. Adjust based on my responses. Start with the first question.
```
