# 7 AI prompts for neurodiverse entrepreneurs

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Productivity & Life Admin** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/7-ai-prompts-for-neurodiverse-entrepreneurs/25290491940556968/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Flow Mode Builder — Design a Focus System That Fits Your Brain

```text
Act as an executive function coach who specializes in neurodiverse entrepreneurs. My background: I run a {business_type} serving {target_audience}. I often struggle with task switching, distractions, and inconsistent motivation. Please design a custom focus framework that helps me enter and maintain flow for {focus_duration} sessions per day. Include structure for breaks, reward systems, and sensory/environment adjustments based on a {preferred_work_environment} setup. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. Money Momentum Map — Simplify Income Streams

```text
Act as a business model strategist for neurodiverse founders. Background: I run a {business_type} that currently earns income through {current_revenue_streams}. I often have too many ideas at once. Create a clear plan to simplify my business model into 1–3 income streams that deliver the most value with minimal context switching. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. Content Creation Neuroflow — Turn Ideas into Simple Systems

```text
Act as a neurodiversity-aware content strategist. My background: I create content for {platforms} to attract {target_audience}, but I get stuck between idea overload and perfectionism. Build me a content creation system with step-by-step templates I can reuse for {product_or_service}. Include prompts for repurposing and automation tools that reduce cognitive load. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. Energy-Based Planning — Schedule That Matches How You Feel

```text
Act as a productivity coach who works with ADHD and autistic entrepreneurs. My background: I work best when {describe_energy_pattern}, and I need flexibility for fluctuating focus. Create a custom weekly plan for my {business_type} that aligns with my energy, sensory needs, and preferred communication style ({communication_preference}). Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. Creative Ideation Grid — Harness Hyperfocus for Innovation

```text
Act as a creative innovation coach for neurodiverse founders. My background: I have strong idea generation skills and tend to hyperfocus on new concepts related to {business_goal}. Build a structured ideation process for capturing, organizing, and validating new ideas for {product_or_service} without losing focus on existing goals. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. Clarity Compass — Decision-Making Without Overwhelm

```text
Act as a clarity and decision-making strategist for neurodiverse entrepreneurs. My background: I often overthink or delay decisions in my {business_type} because I want the "perfect" answer. Create a decision-making template that helps me evaluate options using a simple scorecard based on values, effort, reward, and sensory/mental load. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 7. Support Squad Designer — Build the Right Help Around You

```text
Act as an operations strategist who understands neurodiversity and executive function challenges. My background: I run a {business_type} and I currently handle {list_tasks}. I often feel drained by admin, communication, or repetitive tasks. Help me design a support ecosystem: tasks I can delegate, automate, or outsource first, tools that reduce friction, and suggestions for accountability or body-doubling support. Ask me any questions you have.
```
