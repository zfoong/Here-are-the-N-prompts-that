# 6 ChatGPT prompts that make my schedule feel like I have a top-notch personal assistant

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Productivity & Life Admin** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/6-chatgpt-prompts-that-make-my-schedule-feel-like-i-have-a-top-notch-personal-as/10173689444170347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Smart Weekly Planner

```text
Act as my personal assistant. Based on my goals: [insert goals] and obligations: [insert key tasks/meetings], create a balanced weekly schedule that includes work, rest, learning, and exercise.
```

### 2. Morning Briefing Generator

```text
Every morning, give me a short and punchy daily briefing with my top 3 priorities, any key reminders, and 1 motivating quote or mindset tip. Keep it brief and energizing.
```

### 3. Task Prioritization Coach

```text
Here's my current task list: [paste tasks]. Help me sort this using the Eisenhower Matrix (urgent/important), and suggest what I should delegate, delete, or delay.
```

### 4. Time Block Planner

```text
Create a time-blocked daily schedule for me using the following tasks: [list tasks]. Include breaks, deep work focus time, and buffer zones for flexibility.
```

### 5. Distraction-Proof Workflow

```text
Design a distraction-minimized workflow for me as someone who works from home. Include rules, time slots, focus techniques (like Pomodoro), and a simple habit to stay on track.
```

### 6. Monthly Reset Template

```text
Give me a monthly reflection + planning template. Include sections for what went well, what needs improvement, top lessons, and my 3 priorities for the upcoming month.
```
