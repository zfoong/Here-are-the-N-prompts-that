# 4 Perplexity Prompts for a Full Week of Market Research in 20 Minutes

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.threads.com/@ainspirehub/post/Da8Pirtje2f)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Foundation

```text
What are the key facts and current consensus about [TOPIC]? Include recent developments and cite official sources.
```

### 2. Triangulation

```text
Find 3-5 expert perspectives on [TOPIC]. Where do they agree and disagree?
```

### 3. Recency

```text
What has changed about [TOPIC] in the past month? Only sources after [DATE].
```

### 4. Gaps

```text
What are the major unknowns or gaps in current research on [TOPIC]?
```
