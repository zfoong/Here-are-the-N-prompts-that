# 7 Expert ChatGPT Prompts for Marketing Mode

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/100089314351644/posts/chatgpt-has-a-marketing-mode-and-its-insaneuse-these-7-expert-prompts-to-plan-bu/871521369168334/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Full funnel

```text
Act as my marketing director and create a complete funnel for my business. Include awareness strategy, lead generation assets, nurture sequences, conversion plan, and retention tactics.
```

### 2. 30-day content plan

```text
Based on my niche, build a 30-day content plan with daily post ideas, formats, hooks, and objectives. Optimize it for reach, trust, and conversions.
```

### 3. Brand strategy

```text
Audit my online presence and develop a brand strategy. Include positioning, messaging pillars, tone of voice, visual identity notes, and key differentiators.
```

### 4. Copy package

```text
Create a full copy package for my campaign. Include headline variations, ad copy, landing page copy, email sequence outline, and retargeting angles.
```

### 5. Viral hooks

```text
Generate 50 viral hook ideas tailored to my niche and audience. Make them scroll-stopping, curiosity-driven, and structured for social platforms.
```

### 6. Paid ads strategy

```text
Design a complete paid ads strategy for Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok. Include audiences, creatives, ad scripts, funnel structure, KPIs, and optimization rules.
```

### 7. 12-month calendar

```text
Act as a CMO and create a 12-month marketing calendar with campaigns, themes, launches, seasonal promotions, and weekly execution steps. Make it realistic for a solo operator.
```
