# 10 Hidden ChatGPT Prompts for Growth & Marketing

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/LoganMillerVideos/posts/10-hidden-chatgpt-prompts-they-dont-want-you-to-usetheyre-not-illegalbut-they-fe/944986738495144/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. DECODE WHAT WORKS

```text
Analyze [brand/person]'s content strategy, what patterns, hooks, and tactics are driving their growth?
```

### 2. YOUR AI COPY CHIEF

```text
Generate 10 high-converting headlines for a [product] aimed at [audience].
```

### 3. REMIX WHAT'S VIRAL

```text
Rewrite this viral [tweet/ad/blog] for my niche: [insert description].
```

### 4. COMPETITOR X-RAY

```text
What are [competitor]'s blind spots—and how can I outperform them?
```

### 5. SUCCESS BLUEPRINT

```text
Break down how [person] grew their brand or business. What can I replicate?
```

### 6. OFFER UPGRADE MACHINE

```text
Here's my current offer: [insert]. How can I make it 10x more valuable?
```

### 7. TREND HUNTER

```text
What emerging trends are showing up in [niche]—and how can I ride the wave early?
```

### 8. VALUE POSITIONING PRO

```text
I want to sell [product/service] for [$$$]. Help me position the value so it feels worth every penny.
```

### 9. UNCONVENTIONAL GROWTH MODE

```text
Give me 5 creative ways to grow my [audience/email list] in [X days/weeks].
```

### 10. EFFICIENCY ENGINE

```text
I've got limited [time/money/resources]. How do I get 10x output with AI?
```
