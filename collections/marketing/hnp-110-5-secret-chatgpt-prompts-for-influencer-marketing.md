# 5 secret ChatGPT prompts for influencer marketing

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/5-secret-prompts-of-chatgpt-for-influencer-marketing/10173094676315347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Influencer Collaboration Matchmaker

```text
Identify 10 influencers in [specific niche] who align with a brand targeting [target audience] and have engagement rates above [percentage].
```

### 2. Content Idea Generator for Campaigns

```text
Create 5 innovative content ideas for a [product/service] campaign in collaboration with influencers on [platform, e.g., Instagram or TikTok].
```

### 3. Audience Analysis Deep Dive

```text
Analyze the typical audience demographics for [specific influencer] to ensure they align with our target market of [describe target audience].
```

### 4. Hashtag Strategy Pro

```text
Generate a list of trending hashtags and niche-specific tags for a campaign in the [industry, e.g., fitness, beauty, tech] space to maximize reach.
```

### 5. Influencer ROI Calculator

```text
Provide a framework to calculate the potential ROI of collaborating with influencers in the [specific niche], factoring in costs, reach, and engagement.
```
