# Steal these 5 ChatGPT prompts for TikTok growth

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Growth** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/steal-these-5-chatgpt-prompts-for-tiktok-growth-copy-paste-go-viralno-guesswork-/10173863881465347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Niche Growth Plan

```text
Give me a 30-day TikTok content growth strategy for [niche], including post frequency, video styles, and engagement tactics.
```

### 2. Trending Content Finder

```text
List 10 trending TikTok ideas, sounds, or challenges I can adapt for [niche] this week to increase reach.
```

### 3. Viral Script Generator

```text
Write a 15–30 second TikTok script for [topic] with a hook in the first 3 seconds, engaging storytelling, and a clear call-to-action.
```

### 4. Hashtag Strategy

```text
Give me a list of 20 niche-specific TikTok hashtags that balance high reach and targeted engagement.
```

### 5. Engagement Playbook

```text
Suggest 5 ways to boost comments, shares, and watch time on my TikTok videos in the [niche] industry.
```
