# ✍️ Here are 8 ChatGPT prompts I can literally die for as someone building a personal brand

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Growth** · [Original post ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dorcasfamoriyo_here-are-8-chatgpt-prompts-i-can-literally-activity-7342881506601660417-9zKQ)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Profile audit

```text
Review [PROFILE] for apparent audience, value clarity, visitor confusion, and positioning. Recommend three precise improvements without inventing achievements.
```

### 2. Content calendar

```text
Build a four-week calendar for a [ROLE] with two story posts, one authority post, and one conversation post each week. Provide prompts and inputs, not finished posts.
```

### 3. Niche and audience clarity

```text
Ask me ten questions to define my niche, audience, and unique evidence. Then summarize my answers into one plain positioning statement without buzzwords.
```

### 4. Thought structure

```text
Turn [BRAIN_DUMP] into a hook, context, insight, example, and CTA outline. Do not write the post or add experiences I did not provide.
```

### 5. Performance analysis

```text
Compare [POSTS_AND_METRICS] for hook, credibility, resonance, and audience response. Separate metric observations from causal guesses and suggest tests.
```

### 6. Personal-story reframing

```text
For [REAL_STORY], identify a universal insight, useful lesson, and natural connection to [WORK], without turning it into a forced pitch.
```

### 7. Lead-magnet development

```text
Suggest three small lead magnets for [AUDIENCE] and [PAIN_POINT], scored for usefulness, positioning fit, build effort, and evidence needed.
```

### 8. Positioning correction

```text
Analyze [RECENT_POSTS_AND_OFFERS] for the audience they imply, mismatch with [TARGET_AUDIENCE], and subtle course corrections that preserve continuity.
```
