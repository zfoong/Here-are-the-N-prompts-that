# 8 proven prompts for Facebook growth

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Growth** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/altiam.artificialintelligence/posts/facebook-growth-can-be-simple-copy-these-8-proven-prompts-and-watch-your-page-ex/122179230098620880/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The Viral Format Finder

```text
Act as a Facebook content strategist. Analyze the last 20 posts from pages in my niche and give me 10 post formats proven to go viral, with hooks, angles, and examples I can copy.
```

### 2. The 30-Day Growth Calendar

```text
Create a 30-day Facebook content plan focused on reach + engagement. Include hooks, story angles, carousel ideas, and CTAs for each day.
```

### 3. The Story-to-Post Transformer

```text
Turn this idea into a Facebook-optimized post using the format: Hook → Relatable Story → Sharp Lesson → CTA. Make it bold, conversational, and shareable: {paste your idea}.
```

### 4. The Engagement Booster

```text
Rewrite my Facebook post to maximize comments and saves. Add open loops, curiosity triggers, and punchier sentence flow: {paste post}.
```

### 5. The Hook Factory

```text
Give me 15 Facebook hooks tailored to my niche. Make them bold, scroll-stopping, and built around tension, fear of missing out, or status.
```

### 6. The Short-Form Reel Scriptwriter

```text
Create a Facebook reel script (15–25 seconds) that introduces a problem, offers a quick solution, and ends with a strong CTA. Topic: {your topic}.
```

### 7. The Authority Builder

```text
Generate 10 'personal authority' Facebook posts that show expertise without sounding salesy. Include short stories, credibility triggers, and lessons.
```

### 8. The Viral Condenser

```text
Turn this long-form idea into a short viral Facebook post under 6 lines, optimized for mobile scrolling and shares: {paste your text}.
```
