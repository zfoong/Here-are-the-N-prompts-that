# 10 smart prompts to turn ChatGPT into your personal tutor

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Learning** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/altiamkabir/posts/here-are-10-smart-prompts-to-turn-chatgpt-into-your-personal-tutor-explain-topic/24656849840616029/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Explain like I'm 5

```text
Explain [topic] like I'm 5.
```

### 2. Real-world analogies

```text
Give 3 real-world analogies for [topic].
```

### 3. Stay motivated

```text
Help me stay motivated to learn [topic].
```

### 4. Role-play a scenario

```text
Let's role-play a scenario where I apply [skill].
```

### 5. Study plan

```text
Create a study plan for [topic] over 4 weeks.
```

### 6. Ten-question quiz

```text
Give me a 10-question quiz on [topic].
```

### 7. Mind map

```text
Create a mind map for [topic].
```

### 8. Debate between experts

```text
Simulate a debate between 2 experts on [issue].
```

### 9. Memory tricks

```text
Give me memory tricks to recall [concept].
```

### 10. Improve essay or notes

```text
Improve my essay/notes on [topic] and explain why.
```
