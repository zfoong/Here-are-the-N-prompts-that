# 7 ChatGPT Prompts to Cut a Teacher's Workload in Half

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Learning** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/savingwhiz/posts/teachers-these-7-chatgpt-prompts-can-cut-your-workload-in-half1-design-a-40-minu/1253058290324565/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Lesson plan builder

```text
Design a 40-minute lesson plan on [topic] for [grade level], including objectives, warmup, main activity, and final recap.
```

### 2. Learner-friendly summary

```text
Break down this [chapter/article] into a learner-friendly summary for [grade level].
```

### 3. Quiz generator

```text
Create 12 quiz items (multiple choice + short answer) on [topic] with an answer key.
```

### 4. Creative activities

```text
Recommend 5 creative activities to explain [concept] to [age group].
```

### 5. Parent update

```text
Draft a weekly parent update outlining what students learned in [subject], written in a positive tone.
```

### 6. Differentiated assignments

```text
Create assignments on [topic] at beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels.
```

### 7. Grading rubric

```text
Build a grading rubric for [assignment type] that's clear, balanced, and equitable.
```
