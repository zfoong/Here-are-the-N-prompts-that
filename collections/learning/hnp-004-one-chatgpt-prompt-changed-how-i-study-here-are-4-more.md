# 🧠 One ChatGPT prompt changed how I study. Here are 4 more.

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Learning** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/notioncreations/comments/1rppzm3/one_chatgpt_prompt_that_completely_changed_how_i/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Knowledge-gap questions

```text
Using my notes [NOTES], ask one question at a time to expose missing links and misconceptions. After each answer, explain the gap briefly and choose the next question.
```

### 2. Opposite-thesis advocate

```text
Argue the strongest credible case against my thesis [THESIS]. State assumptions, evidence needed, and which objections would materially weaken my position.
```

### 3. Exam-question generator

```text
From [SYLLABUS_OR_NOTES], create [COUNT] likely exam-style questions across recall, application, and evaluation. Provide a separate answer key and grading criteria.
```

### 4. Five-layer explainer

```text
Explain [CONCEPT] in five layers: child, beginner, practitioner, advanced student, and expert. At each layer, add what the previous layer left out.
```

### 5. Active-recall converter

```text
Turn [NOTES] into concise active-recall questions, cloze cards, and two applied problems. Avoid trivia and prioritize concepts with the highest learning value.
```
