# 7 ChatGPT Prompts You’ll Actually Want to Save

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Prompting** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPT/comments/1k7ulgo)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. 30-Second Email Summarizer

```text
Summarize this messy inbox: [paste email threads].
Grab the deadlines, main points, and anything that's a top priority.
```

### 2. Digital Product Idea Machine

```text
Give me 5 profitable [digital product ideas of specific niches].
I could launch [time duration (this month/ week)] - based on low effort, trending demand, and zero audience.
```

### 3. Imitating a Bestseller

```text
Imitate [famous author]'s style.
Create a complete book plan that includes an outline, chapter guides, and character arcs. Make it ready to publish on Amazon KDP.
```

### 4. Idea Validator

```text
Act like a VC. Test this business idea [paste idea] for profit potential and risks.
Then, find ways to improve it before launching.
```

### 5. Social Media Ghostwriter

```text
Write 10 tweet-sized posts in my voice on [topic]. Include hooks that stop the scroll.
```

### 6. AI Travel Hacker

```text
Plan a 3-day itinerary for [city name] under $500.
Include hidden gems, cheap eats, and best value stays.
```

### 7. Sales Page Optimizer

```text
Rewrite this sales page [paste copy] to drive sales and increase conversions.
Make it punchier, clearer, and create a sense of urgency.
```
