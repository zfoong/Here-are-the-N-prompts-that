# 💼 I tested 20 viral ChatGPT prompts. Here are the 3 that actually worked.

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Prompting** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPT/comments/1sfsdpj/i_tested_20_viral_chatgpt_prompts_here_are_the_3/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Strategic discomfort

```text
Ask me targeted questions about [STALLED_GOAL], identify avoidance patterns without diagnosing me, challenge assumptions, and propose one safe, time-bound discomfort experiment. Never suggest reckless or harmful action.
```

### 2. Learning-strategy diagnostic

```text
Ask ten questions about [SUBJECT], environment, prior results, and constraints. Recommend five evidence-based study techniques and a two-week experiment; avoid fixed learning-style labels.
```

### 3. Business pre-mortem

```text
Treat [BUSINESS_IDEA] as a pre-mortem. Identify three blind spots, five plausible failure modes, competitive vulnerabilities, evidence required, and the single highest-value validation test.
```
