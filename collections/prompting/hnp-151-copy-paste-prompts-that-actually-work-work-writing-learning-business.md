# Copy-paste prompts that actually work (work, writing, learning & business)

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Prompting** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/simplydigital.gr/posts/save-these-copy-paste-prompts-that-actually-work-work-writing-learning-business/1377897074350748/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Work & Professional — Email Rewrite

```text
Rewrite this email so it sounds confident, concise, and professional without being cold. Audience: busy executive. Goal: get a clear yes or no. Here is the draft: [paste email].
```

### 2. Work & Professional — Meeting Transcript Summary

```text
Summarize this meeting transcript into clear action items, owners, and deadlines. Output it as a short internal update I can paste into Slack. Transcript: [paste text].
```

### 3. Work & Professional — Task Breakdown

```text
Act as a senior project manager. Break this vague task into a realistic step-by-step execution plan with timelines and risks. Task: [describe task].
```

### 4. Work & Professional — Persuasive Message

```text
Improve this message so it sounds persuasive but not salesy. Keep it under 120 words. Audience already knows me. Text: [paste].
```

### 5. Work & Professional — SOP Creation

```text
Turn this messy document into a clean SOP that a new hire could follow without asking questions. Keep the language simple and direct. Document: [paste].
```

### 6. Writing & Content — Text Clarity

```text
Rewrite this text to be clearer, more engaging, and easier to skim while keeping the original meaning. Avoid buzzwords. Text: [paste].
```

### 7. Writing & Content — Social Media Post

```text
Turn this idea into a short-form social media post with a strong hook, natural flow, and a memorable closing line. Platform: [TikTok / Instagram / LinkedIn]. Idea: [paste].
```

### 8. Writing & Content — Article Expansion

```text
Expand this rough outline into a complete article with logical flow, short paragraphs, and examples. Target reader is smart but impatient. Outline: [paste].
```

### 9. Writing & Content — Humanize Text

```text
Edit this text to sound more human and conversational without losing authority. Remove anything that sounds robotic. Text: [paste].
```

### 10. Writing & Content — Hook Generation

```text
Create 5 alternative hooks for this content. Each hook should spark curiosity without exaggeration or clickbait. Topic: [paste].
```

### 11. Learning & Skill-Building — Skill Teaching

```text
Teach me [skill] as if I'm smart but starting from zero. Give me the mental models first, then practical steps, then common mistakes. No fluff.
```

### 12. Learning & Skill-Building — Concept Explanation

```text
Explain this concept using a simple analogy first, then a real-world example, then a short summary I can remember. Concept: [paste].
```

### 13. Learning & Skill-Building — Learning Plan

```text
Create a 14-day learning plan for [skill] assuming I have 30 minutes per day. Focus on leverage, not theory.
```

### 14. Learning & Skill-Building — Understanding Test

```text
Test my understanding of this topic by asking me 5 progressively harder questions. Then explain my mistakes. Topic: [paste].
```

### 15. Learning & Skill-Building — Article Summarization

```text
Summarize this long article into the 20% that gives me 80% of the value. Text: [paste].
```

### 16. Business & Strategy — Business Analysis

```text
Act as a practical business strategist. Analyze this idea honestly. List what works, what's risky, and what I'd need to validate first. Idea: [paste].
```

### 17. Business & Strategy — Value Proposition

```text
Turn this service into a clear value proposition that explains who it's for, what problem it solves, and why it's better. Service: [paste].
```

### 18. Business & Strategy — Pricing Strategy

```text
Help me price this offer realistically based on value, effort, and market positioning. Offer details: [paste].
```

### 19. Business & Strategy — Objection Handling

```text
Generate 10 objections a smart buyer would have about this product, then write short, honest responses to each. Product: [paste].
```

### 20. Business & Strategy — Landing Page Structure

```text
Create a simple landing page structure for this offer. Headline, subheadline, sections, and CTA. Keep it clean. Offer: [paste].
```

### 21. Creative & Thinking — Unconventional Angles

```text
Give me 10 unconventional angles for this topic that most people are not talking about. Topic: [paste].
```

### 22. Creative & Thinking — Assumption Challenge

```text
Challenge my assumptions about this idea and suggest better alternatives. Idea: [paste].
```

### 23. Creative & Thinking — Storytelling

```text
Turn this boring explanation into a short story that makes the point memorable. Explanation: [paste].
```

### 24. Creative & Thinking — Content Ideation

```text
Brainstorm 20 content ideas around this theme, ranked from safest to boldest. Theme: [paste].
```
