# 'Illegal' ChatGPT prompts you can't ignore

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Prompting** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/illegal-chatgpt-prompts-you-cant-ignoretheyre-not-actually-illegal-just-insanely/10173738589990347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The "Clone Their Strategy" Prompt

```text
Break down the online content strategy of [insert competitor/influencer]. What platforms do they use, what type of content performs best, and how could I ethically adapt their playbook?
```

### 2. The "Turn 1 Idea into 10" Prompt

```text
Here's a topic I want to create content about: [insert]. Give me 10 high-performing angles, formats, or hooks for different platforms — short-form, long-form, and visual.
```

### 3. The Passive Income Starter Prompt

```text
I want to build passive income with low startup costs. Based on my skills/interests in [insert], give me 3 ideas, plus how to monetize each one in 30–90 days.
```

### 4. The Ultra-Specific Hook Generator

```text
Create 10 scroll-stopping hooks or headlines for a [video/blog/email] on [topic]. Make them specific, curiosity-driven, and designed to get clicks.
```

### 5. The "Write It Like a Pro" Prompt

```text
Rewrite this like a high-converting landing page/email/tweet — persuasive, clear, and focused on benefits, not features. Target audience: [insert].
```

### 6. The 5-Minute Expert Prompt

```text
Explain [insert complex topic] in simple terms like you're teaching a smart 12-year-old. Use analogies or stories to make it unforgettable.
```

### 7. The Automation Blueprint Prompt

```text
I want to save 10 hours a week. Suggest tasks I can automate (content, client onboarding, outreach, etc.) — and the tools to do it with step-by-step setup.
```
