# 💼 Here are 7 prompts that unlock God Mode in ChatGPT

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Prompting** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/chatgpt-has-a-secret-modeit-can-think-like-a-godnot-for-busyworknot-for-basic-ta/10173818751415347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Zero-resource founder plan

```text
Given my verified skills [SKILLS], resources [RESOURCES], and market [MARKET], propose three businesses I can cheaply test. Include assumptions, legal/ethical constraints, and a seven-day validation plan—not a $10M fantasy.
```

### 2. Automation audit

```text
Map my workflow [WORKFLOW] and identify tasks suitable for automation, delegation, or retention. Score reliability, privacy, cost, exception rate, and human-review needs.
```

### 3. Expert mental-model study

```text
From [PRIMARY_SOURCES] about [EXPERT], extract documented decision principles, examples, and contradictions. Do not impersonate or claim access to private thinking.
```

### 4. Income scenario planner

```text
To explore [INCOME_GOAL] by [DATE], calculate required economics, constraints, failure modes, and ethical options. Challenge feasibility and propose evidence-gathering steps.
```

### 5. Routine leverage audit

```text
Audit [CALENDAR_AND_GOALS] for alignment, energy, deep work, obligations, and recovery. Recommend small changes with trade-offs; skip military cosplay.
```

### 6. Knowledge-to-offer map

```text
Inventory my demonstrated knowledge [EXPERIENCE_AND_EVIDENCE], map problems it could help solve, and propose three low-cost offers with validation and scope boundaries.
```

### 7. Devil's-advocate review

```text
Challenge [STRATEGY] by identifying missing evidence, avoided trade-offs, incentives, failure modes, and disconfirming tests; finish with the strongest surviving case.
```
