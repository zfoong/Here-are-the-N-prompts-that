# I unlocked expert mode in ChatGPT: 6 prompts

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Prompting** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/i-unlocked-expert-mode-in-chatgpthere-are-6-prompts-that-gave-me-strategies-and-/10174363093660347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Strategic Insight Generator

```text
ChatGPT, act as a strategist with 10+ years of experience in business and digital marketing. Analyze my current goals and suggest unconventional, high-leverage strategies to grow faster.
```

### 2. Blind Spot Identifier

```text
ChatGPT, review my current approach to [insert project/goal] and identify the blind spots, missed opportunities, and risky assumptions I might not be seeing.
```

### 3. Framework Builder

```text
ChatGPT, create a custom decision-making framework that helps me evaluate new ideas or projects efficiently, based on risk, ROI, and alignment with my long-term goals.
```

### 4. Competitive Edge Finder

```text
ChatGPT, research the top 5 players in my niche or industry. Then tell me what they're not doing that I can capitalize on to stand out.
```

### 5. Systems Thinker Prompt

```text
ChatGPT, map out how I can turn my current process into a repeatable system—one that can scale, automate, and reduce time spent on repetitive work.
```

### 6. Future-Focused Innovation Prompt

```text
ChatGPT, forecast the next 12–24 months in [your industry/niche]. What trends or shifts should I prepare for to stay ahead of competitors?
```
