# 6 prompts for studio-level Canva designs

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Design** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/altiam.artificialintelligence/posts/still-dragging-boxes-in-canva-for-hours-steal-these-6-prompts-and-g%D0%B5t-studio-lev/122176963610620880/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Project Snapshot in 30 seconds

```text
Act as a senior brand designer. Ask me 5 quick questions that let you draft a one-page brief for a [poster / social ad / website hero].
```

### 2. Instant Moodboard Variations

```text
Using the brief above, list 6 distinct visual directions with color palettes, reference links, and key adjectives.
```

### 3. Layout Wireframe Blueprint

```text
Act as a UX lead. Produce an ASCII wireframe for a 1080 × 1350 carousel slide that highlights the main headline, subhead, image area, and CTA.
```

### 4. Color & Typeface Picker

```text
Based on the brand moodboard, choose one primary color, two secondary colors, and two Google Fonts that maximise accessibility and contrast. Explain why in 3 sentences.
```

### 5. On-Brand Social Graphic in One Shot

```text
Write hex codes, font sizes, and hierarchy guidelines to turn the wireframe into a finished Instagram post. Include suggested copy with a maximum of 15 words headline and 20 words body text.
```

### 6. Final Export QA Checklist

```text
Generate a pre-flight checklist for exporting this design to JPG, PNG, PDF, and MP4, including resolution, compression targets, and naming conventions.
```
