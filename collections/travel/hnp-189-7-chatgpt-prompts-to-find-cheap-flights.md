# 7 ChatGPT prompts to find cheap flights

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Travel** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/Owwstin/posts/goodbye-skyscanner-goodbye-google-flightsbooked-a-1120-flight-for-just-142no-mil/10160962801551256/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Find Hidden Flight Deals

```text
You're a travel hacker. Find me 5 international flight deals from [Your City] under $300 in the next 60 days. Prioritize major cities, minimal layovers, and hidden fare tactics.
```

### 2. Trigger Price Drops

```text
Build a plan to track price changes for flights to [Destination] using free tools and alerts. Include steps for catching flash sales and off-peak rates.
```

### 3. Bypass Expensive Dates

```text
Act like a flight analyst. Show me the cheapest round-trip date combos from [City] to [City] in [Month], focusing on weekdays and flexible returns.
```

### 4. Beat the System

```text
You're a former airline insider. Share 3 overlooked booking tricks that can instantly lower airfare.
```

### 5. Optimize Airport Choices

```text
List cheaper alternate airports near [City], and compare average fares. Include travel time and cost to each one.
```

### 6. Leverage Stopovers

```text
Find international flights with long layovers I can turn into bonus trips. Focus on Europe or Asia with 20+ hour stopovers.
```

### 7. Build My Travel Bot

```text
You're a developer. Give me a basic automation idea using free tools (like Google Alerts or Zapier) to track cheap flights to 3 destinations I choose.
```
