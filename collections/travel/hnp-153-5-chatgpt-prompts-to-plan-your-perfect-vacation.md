# 5 ChatGPT prompts to plan your perfect vacation

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Travel** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/Owwstin/posts/these-5-chatgpt-prompts-create-your-perfect-vacation-in-minutes-not-months-and-s/10160909397576256/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Budget-Friendly Itinerary Generator

```text
Build a 5-day travel itinerary for [destination] under [your budget]. Include hidden gems, restaurants, and transportation.
```

### 2. Custom Packing List

```text
I'm traveling to [destination] for [number] days. Generate a detailed packing list based on weather, activities, and local culture.
```

### 3. Local Foodie Recommendations

```text
List the top 5 local dishes I must try in [destination] and recommend authentic places locals actually love.
```

### 4. Instant Language Essentials

```text
Teach me 5 must-know phrases in [language] to navigate restaurants, shopping, and transportation.
```

### 5. Travel Safety Briefing

```text
Provide a concise, practical safety guide for traveling solo in [destination]. Include common scams and tips to stay safe.
```
