## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Secret Flight Finder

```text
Find the cheapest route from [city A] to [city B] next month, including hidden routes and lesser-known airlines.
```

### 2. Local Insider Guide

```text
Give me 10 underrated places to eat, see, and stay in [destination] that locals actually love.
```

### 3. Packing Minimalist

```text
Create the ultimate carry-on packing list for a 7-day trip to [destination], keeping it minimalist but practical.
```

### 4. Perfect Day Planner

```text
Plan a perfect 24-hour itinerary in [destination] that avoids tourist traps and crowds.
```

### 5. Language Crash Course

```text
Teach me 10 essential phrases in [local language] to confidently navigate basic situations on my trip.
```
