# The prompts airlines don't want you to know: 7 ChatGPT prompts that saved me hundreds on flights + hotels

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Travel** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/the-prompts-airlines-dont-want-you-to-know-%EF%B8%8F7-chatgpt-prompts-that-saved-me-hund/10174015022470347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Fare Sniper

```text
Act like a travel agent who tracks hidden deals. Compare flight prices across [airlines] for [destination] on [dates]. Tell me which days are cheapest, and why.
```

### 2. Layover Optimizer

```text
Find flights from [origin] to [destination] where a layover city doubles as a mini-trip. Suggest what I can see/do during that stopover.
```

### 3. Fare Hack Coach

```text
Explain how to use flexible dates + nearby airports to cut the cost of flying from [city] to [destination]. Include exact examples and price ranges.
```

### 4. Hotel Upgrade Script

```text
Write me a polite, persuasive message I can send to a hotel asking for a free upgrade for a special occasion (birthday/anniversary/work trip).
```

### 5. Travel Rewards Maximizer

```text
Based on these loyalty points [insert details], what's the smartest way to book flights and hotels for [trip details] to maximize value?
```

### 6. Local Pricing Advantage

```text
Show me if flights from [neighboring country/city] to [destination] are cheaper than from [my city]. Include cost of positioning flight.
```

### 7. Smart Packing & Saving

```text
Build me a packing list for a [length] trip to [destination] with weather considerations, so I avoid extra baggage fees.
```
