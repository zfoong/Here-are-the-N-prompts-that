# 💸 I gave ChatGPT my salary and it fixed my finances. Just 7 prompts.

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Money Management** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/i-gave-chatgpt-my-salary-and-it-fixed-my-financesno-spreadsheetsno-budgeting-app/10173643573570347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Zero-based budget

```text
Using after-tax income [INCOME], required expenses [FIXED], variable spending [VARIABLE], debts [DEBTS], and goals [GOALS], draft a zero-based monthly budget and show every calculation.
```

### 2. 50/30/20 comparison

```text
Apply the 50/30/20 guideline to [INCOME], then compare it with my actual essentials and obligations. Explain where the rule does not fit and propose a realistic alternative.
```

### 3. Cash-flow tracker

```text
Design a monthly cash-flow table for [INCOME_SOURCES], [BILLS], [VARIABLE_CATEGORIES], and [DATES], including opening balance, due dates, actuals, variance, and closing balance.
```

### 4. Savings target

```text
Calculate the monthly amount needed to reach [GOAL_AMOUNT] by [DATE] from [CURRENT_SAVINGS], using explicitly stated return assumptions and a zero-return baseline.
```

### 5. Weekly money check-in

```text
Create a ten-minute weekly review covering balances, upcoming bills, unusual spending, debt, savings progress, and one next action.
```

### 6. Beginner investing questions

```text
Explain a three-step beginner investing research process for [COUNTRY], [GOAL], [TIME_HORIZON], and [RISK_TOLERANCE], emphasizing fees, diversification, taxes, and professional advice.
```

### 7. Monthly money system

```text
Combine [BUDGET], [TRACKER], and [GOALS] into a repeatable monthly checklist with dates, reminders, review triggers, and an exception process.
```
