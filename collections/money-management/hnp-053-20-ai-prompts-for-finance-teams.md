# The 20 Claude & ChatGPT prompts that separate elite finance teams from everyone else.

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Money Management** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/UnaAI/comments/1srnqwg/the_20_claude_chatgpt_prompts_that_separate_elite)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Excel Formulas

```text
I'm working in Excel with two sheets: one has sales order & order amount, the other has sales orders with customer names & dates. I need a formula to fetch the customer name in the first sheet using sales order as the key. Give me the exact formula and explain how to use it.
```

### 2. Create PPT with AI

```text
I'm a CFO preparing our monthly review presentation. Draft the outline of our slide deck, starting with executive summary, commercial update, P&L, Cash and Risks/Opportunities. Each slide should have 3 bullet points. Write the commentaries like a seasoned CFO.
```

### 3. Python + Analysis

```text
My data is in an Excel File called 'Sales 2025'. I am using Google Colab. Generate the full Python code to identify outliers and show me a geographical dashboard. Here are the headers and first 5 lines as example: [insert data example here].
```

### 4. Deep Research

```text
I'm a FP&A manager working in SaaS. I need to perform a benchmark analysis. Perform the analysis based on US data for SaaS company in Fintech around $20 to $50M revenue. Add a table as summary of the analysis.
```

### 5. Financial Analysis

```text
I'm the finance manager of a beverage group. Based on the dataset attached, give me 5 key bullet points and 5 areas to investigate. First perform data consistency check. Then the analysis. Show me your calculations. Do this analysis like an experienced CFO in our industry.
```

### 6. Scenario Analysis

```text
I'm in FP&A. In Jan, we sold $183k of equipment (8,451 units). I need help modeling 2 pricing scenarios: a 5% increase and a 5% decrease, using a demand elasticity of -1.3. Show new price, quantity, and sales. Show me your calculations and present the result in a table format. Act as the best modeler.
```

### 7. Budget Instructions

```text
I am the finance manager. I need to draft the instructions for this year's budget. Help me create the instructions for all departments and for the finance team. It needs to include objectives, key assumptions, time schedule and deliverables. Ask me for information you need before starting and I will provide them.
```

### 8. Set Up Action Plan

```text
I'm an FP&A analyst. Sales came in 15% below budget due to supply chain delays. Can you draft a bullet-point action plan I can present to management to help us close the gap? Make it solution-oriented, measurable, & structured like a proactive finance business partner.
```

### 9. Financial Statement Notes

```text
I'm preparing financial statements under US GAAP. Can you draft a note for property, plant, and equipment with a table by category showing cost, depreciation, and net value? Also include useful lives by category. Use a clear, compliant format like a Big 4 partner would.
```

### 10. Scenario Modeling

```text
I want to create a model for a fashion retailer. Create a dynamic model in an Excel file with three different scenarios showing how profit changes based on sales per store, margin, and fixed costs. Make sure to add Excel formulas and link all tables. Build this like the best Excel modeler.
```

### 11. ROI Analysis

```text
I'm reviewing investment initiatives and need an ROI analysis. Calculate ROI, assess financial effectiveness, and give recommendations to optimize future decisions. Before starting, ask me for the necessary data. Act as the best investment analyst.
```

### 12. Write Email Request

```text
I work in finance and need inputs from other departments on 2026 external service needs for our budget. Help me write a clear email request and create a sample table form they can fill. Deadline is June 30, 2025. Use a professional tone — like a finance coordinator gathering inputs.
```

### 13. Advanced Modeling

```text
I'm a young finance professional starting a new role. Teach me advanced financial modeling skills — what they are, why they matter, & how to learn and apply them on the job. Break it down clearly, like a mentor training someone for high-performance FP&A work.
```

### 14. Scenario Planning

```text
I'm creating a visual called '2025 Financial Forecast Scenarios'. Show 3 cases side-by-side: Best ($24M rev, $6M EBITDA), Base ($20M, $4.5M), Downturn ($14M, breakeven). Use horizontal bars with green, gray, & red, plus icons. Make it clear & exec-ready using the best Financial Storytelling techniques.
```

### 15. Cash Flow Projection

```text
I'm building a 12-month cash flow projection for a small retail business. Include beginning cash, inflows, outflows, and ending balance. Present it in a simple, table format. Make it clear and easy to understand for a business owner by using Excel graphs.
```

### 16. Dunning Process

```text
I'm managing receivables and need 3 dunning letter templates: first notice, reminder, and final warning before legal action. Each should include placeholders for invoice number & amount. Tone must escalate with each letter — like a finance pro handling collections professionally.
```

### 17. Cost Reduction

```text
I'm a financial analyst at a manufacturing company. My manager asked for a cost reduction plan. Create a table with the best low hanging fruits actions. Add in one column a 5-step mini plan. Keep it practical, valuable & structured, like the best cost-cutter consultant would do.
```

### 18. Interview Preparation

```text
I'm a finance business partner meeting the head of sales & marketing for the first time. Suggest 10 smart questions I can ask to build trust, understand their priorities, and show how finance can support growth — like a strategic partner starting a high-impact relationship.
```

### 19. Commentaries w/ AI

```text
I'm the CFO of a fashion retailer. Help me draft board commentaries based on the 3 tabs in the file (P&L, Revenue details and Cost details). Keep it professional, bullet-based, highlight key figures, and use strong financial storytelling. Write like a seasoned CFO.
```

### 20. Create an SOP

```text
I'm an accountant using QuickBooks. I need a step-by-step SOP for bank reconciliation that complies with standard internal controls. Make it clear, structured, and practical — like a finance team documenting clean processes for an audit-ready company.
```
