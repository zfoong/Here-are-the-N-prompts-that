# 8 powerhouse prompts to grow your Facebook page

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/SimplyDigital.gr/posts/here-are-8-powerhouse-prompts-you-can-plug-straight-into-chatgpt-to-grow-your-fa/1399050515568737/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The Scroll-Stopping Hook Generator

```text
I want 15 scroll-stopping hooks for a Facebook post. The topic is: [insert topic]. Write them in a punchy, curiosity-driven style that forces a pause in the feed. Keep each hook 1–2 lines max. Avoid hype words like insane or guaranteed. Make them feel like something real creators would use, not generic marketing lines. Prioritise tension, contrast, bold statements, and pattern interrupts.
```

### 2. The Viral Facebook Idea Generator (AI-Related)

```text
Give me 20 viral Facebook post ideas about AI. They should be practical, surprising, based on real-world use cases, and easy for everyday people to relate to. Prioritise ideas that spark strong reactions like curiosity, disagreement, I need to try this, or nobody told me this. Each idea should include a title, a one-sentence angle, and why it would go viral in one line. Make sure they feel native to Facebook, not LinkedIn.
```

### 3. The High-Converting Lead Gen Angle Generator

```text
I need 12 Facebook post angles that naturally generate leads for [insert your service/product] without sounding salesy. Build angles around transformations, mistakes to avoid, behind-the-scenes content, step-by-step processes, I tried this so you don't have to, and mini case studies. Each angle should include a soft CTA that invites people to comment or DM for more details.
```

### 4. The Facebook Algorithm Expert Optimizer

```text
Act as a Facebook algorithm strategist. I'll paste a post. You will analyse it and rewrite it for maximum reach, improve the hook, add story elements where needed, adjust the structure for retention, suggest the ideal CTA, and tell me exactly what makes the optimized version work based on 2025 engagement signals including watch time, comment depth, share intention, dwell time, and re-engagement signals. Give me the optimized post plus a breakdown.
```

### 5. The Controversial Opinion Generator

```text
I want 15 bold opinion-style Facebook posts about [insert topic]. The goal is to spark comments and healthy debate without violating platform policies. Each post should challenge a common belief or industry norm. Keep each one under 5 lines. Make them feel personal and experience-based, not preachy. End each post with a question that invites disagreement or perspective-sharing.
```

### 6. The Story-Based Authority Builder

```text
Give me 10 Facebook post ideas that build authority through storytelling in the niche of [insert niche]. Each idea should include a strong opening line, the core story angle, the lesson, and a soft CTA that encourages comments. Focus on vulnerability, lessons learned, mistakes, or unexpected wins. Make them feel human and relatable, not polished corporate content.
```

### 7. The Comment Magnet Post Builder

```text
Create 15 Facebook posts designed purely to maximize comments in the niche of [insert niche]. Use formats such as this or that, agree or disagree, fill in the blank, what would you choose, and unpopular opinion. Keep them short and native to Facebook. Make them easy to answer in one sentence so friction is low.
```

### 8. The 7-Part Facebook Series Growth Builder

```text
Help me design a 7-part Facebook content series around [insert topic]. Each post should have a clear theme, build on the previous post, tease the next part, and include a reason for people to follow or turn on notifications. Make the structure addictive and anticipation-driven. Suggest a naming format for the series that makes people recognize it instantly in the feed.
```
