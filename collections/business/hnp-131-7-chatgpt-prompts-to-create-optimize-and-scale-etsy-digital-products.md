# 7 ChatGPT prompts to create, optimize, and scale Etsy digital products

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/theaiempire/posts/chatgpt-etsy-use-these-7-prompts-to-create-optimize-and-scale-digital-products-t/122147579420733053/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Digital Product Ideation Prompt

```text
Give me 15 profitable digital product ideas I can create and sell on Etsy using ChatGPT. Focus on low-cost, high-demand niches like templates, planners, Notion dashboards, and AI prompt packs. Include the target audience, estimated price, and reason for demand.
```

### 2. Product Creation Prompt

```text
Act as a digital product creator. Help me write, design, and structure a [type of product—e.g., Notion template, digital planner, eBook, course workbook]. Give me the step-by-step layout, text sections, color palette ideas, and what to include on each page for maximum value.
```

### 3. Listing Optimization Prompt

```text
Write a perfect Etsy product listing for my [product type]. Include an SEO-optimized title, 13 tags, an engaging description, and 3 bullet points that highlight benefits, not features. Make it sound natural and customer-focused.
```

### 4. Visual Branding Prompt

```text
Give me design ideas for my Etsy digital products so they look cohesive and premium. Include brand colors, typography style, and thumbnail layout ideas for a modern and clean aesthetic that converts browsers into buyers.
```

### 5. Upsell & Bundle Strategy Prompt

```text
Help me create 3 bundle offers or upsells related to my main Etsy product to increase average order value. Suggest pricing tiers and how to position each one in the store.
```

### 6. Marketing & Traffic Prompt

```text
Outline a 7-day content plan to promote my Etsy digital products using X (Twitter), Instagram, and Pinterest. Include post ideas, call-to-actions, and how to naturally drive traffic to my Etsy store without sounding salesy.
```

### 7. Scale & Automation Prompt

```text
Help me build a simple automation system to scale my Etsy business using AI tools. Include how to use ChatGPT for copy, Canva for design, and Notion or Airtable for managing listings, analytics, and customer messages efficiently.
```
