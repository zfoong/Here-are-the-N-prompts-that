# From idea to plan: 5 ChatGPT prompts that build your business plan

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/from-idea-to-plan-5-chatgpt-prompts-that-build-your-business-plan-/10173508676305347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Build My Business Plan Outline

```text
Act as a startup consultant. Create a complete business plan outline for a [describe your business idea], including sections like executive summary, market analysis, marketing strategy, operations, and financial projections.
```

### 2. Analyze My Market and Audience

```text
Conduct a market analysis for a business that offers [product/service] in the [industry] space. Identify my ideal customer, competitors, and current market trends.
```

### 3. Map Out My Revenue Model

```text
Suggest 3 revenue streams and pricing strategies for my business idea: [describe your product/service]. Include pros, cons, and monetization potential.
```

### 4. Find My Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

```text
Help me define a strong, memorable USP for my business that solves [specific customer problem] and clearly stands out from competitors in [industry].
```

### 5. Turn My Idea Into a 12-Month Roadmap

```text
Break down my business plan into a 12-month action plan with monthly milestones for launching, marketing, growing, and scaling my business idea.
```
