# I asked ChatGPT to build me a $10K/month side hustle plan: 7 prompts

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/tyler.wise.31105/posts/i-asked-chatgpt-to-build-me-a-10kmonth-side-hustle-planuse-these-7-prompts-to-de/1337515314789116/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Skill-to-Money Mapper

```text
Based on my skills [skills], experience [background], and available time [hours per week], identify realistic side hustle models that can reach [income goal] per month.
```

### 2. Market Demand Validator

```text
For this idea [side hustle idea], analyze real market demand. Who is paying, what they're paying for, and why existing solutions fail or fall short.
```

### 3. High-Value Offer Architect

```text
Help me design a clear, irresistible offer for [target audience] that solves [specific problem]. Include pricing logic, value stack, and why it's worth the price.
```

### 4. Fast Validation Plan

```text
Create a 14-day validation plan for [offer] using [platform] with minimal setup. Focus on proof of demand, not perfection.
```

### 5. Acquisition Strategy Builder

```text
Design a simple customer acquisition system for [side hustle] using [traffic source]. Include daily actions, messaging angle, and conversion goal.
```

### 6. Scale Without Burnout

```text
Show me how to scale [side hustle] from [current income] to [target income] per month without increasing my workload proportionally.
```

### 7. Risk & Reality Check

```text
Act as a brutally honest mentor. Analyze this plan [summary of plan] and tell me what will break first, what's unrealistic, and how to fix it.
```
