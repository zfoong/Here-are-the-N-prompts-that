# 8 AI prompts for strategic partnership architecture

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/8-ai-prompts-for-strategic-partnership-architecture/26101637562775731/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The "Audience-Adjacent" Matchmaker

```text
Act as a Strategic Alliances Director. [Context: My business is [Insert Business Description] and our current weakness is [Insert Weakness]. We want to find partners who already have the trust of our target audience, [Insert Target Audience], but do not offer a competing product.] [Task: Identify 5 "Audience-Adjacent" industries. For each industry, explain why their customers are a perfect fit for us and propose a "Value-Exchange" deal (e.g., a co-branded webinar or a bundled digital resource) that requires no equity.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. The Infrastructure Leverage Specialist

```text
Act as a Business Development Consultant. [Context: We are looking to scale [Insert Product/Service] but we lack the [Insert Weakness, e.g., distribution network/sales team/physical locations].] [Task: Find 3 types of established companies that have "Excess Capacity" in their distribution or marketing channels. Design a "Plug-and-Play" partnership where we provide our product as a value-add to their existing customers in exchange for a percentage of the revenue (RevShare) rather than equity.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. The Certification & Trust Builder

```text
Act as a Brand Authority Strategist. [Context: Our main weakness is [Insert Weakness, e.g., being a new player in a skeptical market]. We need to borrow authority from established institutions.] [Task: Identify 3 non-obvious non-profits, trade associations, or educational institutions that align with our mission. Propose a partnership where we provide [Insert Your Expertise/Data] to their members in exchange for an official "Endorsement" or "Preferred Provider" status.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. The Software Integration (SaaS) Scout

```text
Act as a SaaS Ecosystem Architect. [Context: Our business provides [Insert Service]. We want to integrate into the software stacks used by [Insert Target Audience]. Our current weakness is [Insert Weakness].] [Task: List 5 software tools that our target audience uses daily. For the top 2, design a "Workflow Integration" where our service is triggered by a specific action inside their software, and outline how we can pitch this to the software's API or Business Development team.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. The Reverse-Referral Engine

```text
Act as a Revenue Operations Expert. [Context: Because of our weakness in [Insert Weakness], we often have to turn away leads that are [Insert Reason, e.g., too small/too big/wrong industry]. We are currently making $0 from these "unqualified" leads.] [Task: Identify 3 partner businesses that specialize exactly in our weakness. Design a "Reciprocal Referral Agreement" where we pass them our "unfit" leads for a lead-generation fee, and they do the same for us when they encounter clients needing [Insert Your Primary Strength].] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. The "Influencer-Expert" Hybrid Deal

```text
Act as a Growth Marketer. [Context: We need more leads for [Insert Business], but we have a weakness in [Insert Weakness, e.g., ad budget].] [Task: Identify the specific "Micro-Influencer" niches where our target audience hangs out. Write a partnership pitch that offers them a "Lifetime Commission" on any customers they bring in, rather than a one-time fee, to ensure they are incentivized to promote us long-term.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 7. The Content Co-Creation Magnet

```text
Act as a Content Marketing Strategist. [Context: We want to be seen as leaders in [Insert Industry], but our weakness is [Insert Weakness, e.g., low visibility].] [Task: Propose a "Collaborative Research Project" or "Industry Report" concept. List 5 non-competing experts we can interview for this project. Explain how we will use their participation to get them to share the final result with their massive combined audiences.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 8. The Upsell Partner Bridge

```text
Act as a Customer Journey Architect. [Context: Our product, [Insert Product], is just one step in a larger process. After customers use us, they usually need help with [Insert Next Task]. Our weakness is that we don't provide that next step.] [Task: Find 3 businesses that provide the "Next Step" for our customers. Design a "Post-Purchase Bundle" where our customers get a special deal on their service, and they offer a "Pre-Purchase" deal on ours to their incoming clients.] Ask me any questions you have.
```
