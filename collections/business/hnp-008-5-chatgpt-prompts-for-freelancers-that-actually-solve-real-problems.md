# 💼 5 ChatGPT prompts for freelancers that actually solve real problems

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPTPromptGenius/comments/1s8fy27/5_chatgpt_prompts_for_freelancers_that_actually/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Late-invoice chase

```text
Write a short payment follow-up for invoice [NUMBER], [AMOUNT], due [DATE], at follow-up [STAGE]. State facts, set [NEW_DEADLINE], and end with one clear action.
```

### 2. Price objection

```text
Reply to a client who says [RATE] is too expensive. Defend the value with [EVIDENCE], offer a reduced-scope option or ask for budget, and do not discount automatically.
```

### 3. Ghosted mid-project

```text
Follow up after [DAYS] days of silence on [PROJECT]. Request [NEEDED_INPUT], state the contractual consequence after [DATE], and keep the tone factual.
```

### 4. Specific cold pitch

```text
Write a sub-[WORD_LIMIT]-word pitch from a [FREELANCE_ROLE] to [BUSINESS]. Lead with [OBSERVATION], offer [RESULT], and end with a low-friction question; ban stock outreach phrases.
```

### 5. Out-of-scope request

```text
Respond to a request for [EXTRA_WORK] beyond [AGREED_SCOPE]. Acknowledge it, explain the boundary, and offer a paid change at [RATE_OR_QUOTE_PROCESS].
```
