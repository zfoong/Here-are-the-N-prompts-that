# ChatGPT + affiliate marketing: use these 5 prompts to get started

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/Owwstin/posts/chatgpt-affiliate-marketing-m0ney-while-you-sleepuse-these-5-prompts-to-get-star/10160511849886256/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Affiliate Product Research Prompt

```text
Identify 5 high-converting affiliate products in [NICHE] with commissions over 30%.
```

### 2. Authority Content Funnel

```text
Design a simple content funnel that → attracts → nurtures → converts readers into buyers.
```

### 3. Affiliate Blog Blueprint

```text
Create 10 blog post topics that naturally recommend my affiliate products.
```

### 4. Email Nurture Sequence for Affiliates

```text
Write a 5-part email series that builds trust and lightly pitches affiliate offers.
```

### 5. CTA Insertion Strategy

```text
Suggest ways to add affiliate CTAs naturally into content without sounding desperate.
```
