# Turn any idea into a digital product with these 9 prompts

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/simplydigital.gr/posts/turn-any-idea-into-a-digital-product-with-these-9-promptscopy-and-paste-below/1449242297216225/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Idea Validation

```text
I have this rough idea: [X]. Ask me 5 questions that will tell me if it's worth turning into a digital product.
```

### 2. Product Definition

```text
Turn this idea [X] into a digital product concept. Give me the format, target audience, price point, and one-line value proposition.
```

### 3. Instant Roadmap

```text
Create a 7-day action plan to go from idea to sellable digital product for someone with no audience and no budget.
```

### 4. Content-to-Product

```text
I've been posting about [topic] for months. What digital products could I already create from the content I've made?
```

### 5. AI as Co-Creator

```text
Act as a digital product strategist. I'll describe my skill or knowledge, and you tell me the 3 most profitable digital products I could build around it.
```

### 6. Pricing Psychology

```text
I'm about to launch a digital product on [topic]. Suggest 3 pricing tiers with what to include in each to maximize conversions.
```

### 7. Launch Copy

```text
Write a sales page outline for a digital product called [X] targeting [audience]. Focus on pain points, transformation, and objection handling.
```

### 8. Repurposing Engine

```text
Take this single idea [X] and show me how to turn it into: a free lead magnet, a paid mini-product, and a premium offer.
```

### 9. Feedback Loop

```text
My digital product is live but not selling. Here's what I have: [description]. Diagnose the problem and suggest 3 fixes.
```
