# 💼 Here are the 10 prompts that made me my first $1k online

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPTPromptGenius/comments/1se4igz/here_are_the_10_prompts_that_made_me_my_first_1k/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Niche problem extractor

```text
Analyze the supplied [AUDIENCE_RESEARCH] for recurring problems around [NICHE]. Rank five problems by frequency, urgency, and willingness to pay; quote the evidence and mark anything inferred.
```

### 2. Product idea validator

```text
Evaluate [PRODUCT_IDEA] for [TARGET_CUSTOMER]. List the core job, existing alternatives, strongest objection, cheapest validation test, and pass/fail signals; do not invent market data.
```

### 3. Product title generator

```text
Generate 20 clear titles for a [PRODUCT_TYPE] that helps [AUDIENCE] achieve [OUTCOME]. Avoid hype, explain the promise in plain language, and shortlist the five most specific options.
```

### 4. Product outline builder

```text
Create a practical outline for [PRODUCT_TYPE] on [TOPIC]. Order sections from quick win to deeper implementation and state the outcome, exercise, and evidence needed for each.
```

### 5. Section content writer

```text
Draft the [SECTION_NAME] section for [AUDIENCE] using [SOURCE_NOTES]. Include one example, one action step, and one limitation; flag facts that need checking.
```

### 6. Prompt pack builder

```text
Turn this workflow [WORKFLOW] into [COUNT] reusable prompts. Give each a purpose, required inputs, constraints, and expected output; use [DESCRIPTIVE_VARIABLES].
```

### 7. Human voice editor

```text
Edit [DRAFT] to match this voice sample [VOICE_SAMPLE]. Preserve meaning, remove clichés and unsupported certainty, and list any substantive change separately.
```

### 8. Resource pack creator

```text
Create a companion resource pack for [PRODUCT] with checklists, templates, and a next-step guide. Keep every item directly tied to the promised outcome.
```

### 9. Validation post writer

```text
Draft a non-promotional community post that asks [TARGET_COMMUNITY] about [PROBLEM]. Include context, three concrete questions, and no fabricated personal story.
```

### 10. Sales hook generator

```text
Write 15 one-line hooks for [OFFER] aimed at [AUDIENCE]. Mix problem, outcome, contrast, and specificity; exclude guarantees and rank the top three with reasons.
```
