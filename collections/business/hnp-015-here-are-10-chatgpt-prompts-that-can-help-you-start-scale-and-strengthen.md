# 💼 Here are 10 ChatGPT prompts that can help you start, scale, and strengthen your business

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sufyanmaan_building-a-business-is-hard-but-with-chatgpt-activity-7336723743529951232-3jjR)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Lean startup test

```text
Apply lean-startup thinking to [IDEA]. State the riskiest assumption, smallest test, target users, success threshold, learning goal, and next iteration.
```

### 2. Value proposition canvas

```text
Build a draft value-proposition canvas for [PRODUCT] and [CUSTOMER] from [RESEARCH]. Mark pains, gains, and jobs unsupported by evidence.
```

### 3. OKR setup

```text
Propose [COUNT] objectives for [PROJECT], each with measurable key results, owner, baseline, deadline, and anti-gaming check.
```

### 4. PEST analysis

```text
Analyze political, economic, social, and technological factors affecting [BUSINESS] in [MARKET]. Cite current sources and distinguish signal from speculation.
```

### 5. Five whys

```text
Facilitate a Five Whys analysis of [PROBLEM]. Ask for evidence at each step, avoid forcing a single cause, and finish with testable root-cause hypotheses.
```

### 6. Customer journey map

```text
Map the journey for [CUSTOMER] using [RESEARCH]: stages, goals, touchpoints, emotions, friction, evidence gaps, and prioritized improvements.
```

### 7. Business model canvas

```text
Draft a business model canvas for [BUSINESS], explicitly listing assumptions, evidence, dependencies, unit-economics questions, and validation tests.
```

### 8. Growth experiments

```text
Suggest ten ethical growth experiments for [PRODUCT] and [AUDIENCE], ranked by impact, confidence, effort, cost, measurement, and stopping rule.
```

### 9. Theory of constraints

```text
Identify the likely constraint in [SYSTEM] from [DATA], propose a way to confirm it, then exploit, subordinate, elevate, and recheck the constraint.
```

### 10. Risk plan

```text
Create a risk register for [BUSINESS] with likelihood, impact, leading indicator, mitigation, contingency, owner, and review cadence.
```
