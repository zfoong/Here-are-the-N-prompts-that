# These 10 ChatGPT prompts feel illegal (but they work)

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/these-10-chatgpt-prompts-feel-illegal-but-they-workfor-going-viral-growing-fast-/10173818960580347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Scroll-stopping hooks

```text
Act like a viral growth expert. Give me 5 scroll-stopping hook ideas for a post on [your niche].
```

### 2. Bold opinion post

```text
Write a bold opinion post on [topic] that's short, sharp, and stirs the pot.
```

### 3. 14-day digital product

```text
What digital product could I launch in 14 days for under $10 — with no followers?
```

### 4. Post audit

```text
Audit my last 3 posts and explain why they flopped or flew — then suggest improvements.
```

### 5. 3-part content series

```text
Take this single idea and turn it into a trust-building 3-part content series.
```

### 6. Story to offer

```text
Help me tell a personal story that naturally leads into my offer — without sounding like a pitch.
```

### 7. 60-sec video plan

```text
Act like a short-form video coach. Plan out a 60-sec video that educates, entertains, and sells.
```

### 8. Audience growth comparison

```text
Compare 3 ways to grow an audience on [platform], and tell me which one fits my style best.
```

### 9. 5-day posting plan

```text
Build me a 5-day posting plan that gets comments, DMs, and leads — without burnout.
```

### 10. High-converting CTA

```text
Write a CTA that gets clicks and conversions — without sounding like a robot.
```
