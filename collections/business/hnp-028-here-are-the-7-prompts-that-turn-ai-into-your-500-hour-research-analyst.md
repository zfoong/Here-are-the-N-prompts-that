# 💼 Here are the 7 prompts that turn AI into your $500/hour research analyst

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/tombilyeu/posts/market-research-firms-charge-30000-for-insights-i-get-from-chatgpt-in-30-minutes/1315054873322312/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Competitor blind spots

```text
Using [COMPETITORS] and dated cited sources, compare product, marketing, and customer communication. Identify unaddressed problems and clearly label hypotheses.
```

### 2. Customer-frustration mining

```text
Analyze supplied or accessible reviews for [PRODUCTS]. Report sample, dates, recurring complaints, desired outcomes, representative short excerpts, and sampling bias.
```

### 3. Pricing-gap detection

```text
Map current published prices in [MARKET] with plan limits and customer segments. Identify possible gaps while accounting for hidden discounts and willingness-to-pay evidence.
```

### 4. Trend intersections

```text
Identify three evidenced trends affecting [INDUSTRY], cite current primary sources, and propose testable opportunity hypotheses at their intersections.
```

### 5. Geographic blind spots

```text
Compare [COMPETITORS] across [REGIONS] using current availability, review, and demand proxies. State data coverage before labeling a region underserved.
```

### 6. Channel-gap analysis

```text
Map acquisition and distribution channels in [INDUSTRY] from cited evidence. Identify underused channels and explain why they may be unsuitable, not merely overlooked.
```

### 7. Adjacent-market transfer

```text
Find adjacent industries with similar customer jobs. Compare their solutions and constraints, then propose three cross-pollination experiments rather than assuming transferability.
```
