# Goodbye PowerPoint: 7 ChatGPT prompts that replace slides, decks and hours

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/theaiempire/posts/goodbye-powerpointhere-are-7-chatgpt-prompts-that-replace-slides-decks-and-hours/122157203786733053/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Presentation Strategist

```text
Act as a senior presentation consultant. Based on my topic, audience, and goal [describe], design a persuasive presentation structure that flows logically and keeps attention.
```

### 2. Storyline Builder

```text
Turn this idea or content [paste] into a strong narrative arc with a clear opening, problem, insight, solution, and memorable ending.
```

### 3. Slide Logic Generator

```text
Convert this presentation outline [paste] into concise slide messages. Each slide should communicate one clear idea in simple language.
```

### 4. Audience Relevance Optimizer

```text
Rewrite this presentation [paste] so every section directly answers what the audience cares about, removes fluff, and increases clarity.
```

### 5. Visual Direction Guide

```text
Suggest visual concepts, charts, and layout ideas that support each section without overloading slides.
```

### 6. Speaker Script Creator

```text
Create short speaking notes for each section so I sound confident, natural, and persuasive instead of reading slides.
```

### 7. Executive Summary Builder

```text
Turn this full presentation [paste] into a powerful 60-second executive summary that captures the core message.
```
