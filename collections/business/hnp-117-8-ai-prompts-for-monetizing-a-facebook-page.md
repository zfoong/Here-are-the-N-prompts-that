# 8 AI prompts for monetizing a Facebook page

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/8-ai-prompts-for-monetizing-a-facebook-page/26212943838311769/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The Affiliate Income Strategy

```text
Act as a Digital Marketing Strategist specializing in affiliate marketing. I have a Facebook page focused on [Insert Niche, e.g., Dog Training] with an audience of [Insert Follower Count] people who are interested in [Insert Top Interest]. I want to promote [Insert Affiliate Product Name] which solves the problem of [Insert Problem Solved]. Create a 5-post content series that educates my audience on this problem and naturally recommends the product as the solution. Include a mix of storytelling, educational tips, and soft-selling. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. The Digital Product Brainstormer

```text
Act as a Product Development Expert. My Facebook audience consists of [Insert Audience Demographics] who frequently ask questions about [Insert Common Questions/Pain Points]. I want to create a low-cost digital product (under $50) that I can sell directly to them. Based on these pain points, generate 5 specific ideas for digital products (like eBooks, templates, or mini-courses) that would solve their problems. For each idea, suggest a catchy title and a key benefit. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. The Email List Builder

```text
Act as a Direct Response Copywriter. I need to move my Facebook followers onto my email newsletter where I can sell my [Insert Product/Service]. I am offering a free [Insert Lead Magnet, e.g., Checklist/PDF] in exchange for their email. Write a high-converting Facebook post that highlights the frustration of [Insert Pain Point] and positions my free resource as the immediate solution. Include a clear Call to Action (CTA) telling them to click the link to download. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. The Sponsorship Pitch Creator

```text
Act as a Public Relations Manager. I want to pitch a partnership to [Insert Brand Name] to sponsor posts on my Facebook page. My engagement rate is roughly [Insert Engagement Rate] and my audience loves content about [Insert Content Topic]. Write a persuasive cold email to the marketing manager of this brand. The email should introduce my page, highlight my audience stats, and propose a specific collaboration idea involving [Insert Idea, e.g., product review video]. Keep it professional but enthusiastic. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. The High-Ticket Service Launch

```text
Act as a Business Coach. I have expertise in [Insert Your Skill/Expertise]. I want to offer a high-ticket coaching or consulting package to my Facebook followers. Help me structure this offer. Create a package name, list 3-5 core deliverables that would provide immense value to [Insert Target Client Description], and suggest a pricing strategy. Then, write a "teaser" post for Facebook that announces I have spots open for this new program. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. The Merchandise Concept Generator

```text
Act as a Creative Brand Director. My Facebook community loves to joke about [Insert Common Niche Joke or Phrase] and we value [Insert Core Value/Vibe, e.g., sarcasm/motivation]. I want to launch a line of simple merchandise (T-shirts, mugs, stickers). Brainstorm 10 short, punchy phrases or design concepts that would resonate with this specific audience. The designs should be something they would be proud to wear or use to show they belong to this group. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 7. The Webinar Sales Script

```text
Act as a Webinar Sales Scriptwriter. I am planning a Facebook Live session to sell [Insert Product Name]. The price is [Insert Price]. Outline a 30-minute live video script. The structure should be: 5 minutes of introduction and hooking the viewer, 15 minutes of teaching valuable content regarding [Insert Teaching Topic], and 10 minutes pitching the product as the next logical step. Include specific cues for when to ask the audience to comment below to keep engagement high. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 8. The "Exclusive Group" Upsell

```text
Act as a Community Manager. I want to start a paid VIP membership group off of Facebook (like on Patreon or Skool) for my "super fans." Currently, I post [Insert Current Content Strategy] for free. List 5 exclusive perks or premium content ideas I could offer in a paid group that would justify a [Insert Monthly Price] subscription fee. Focus on things that save them time or give them closer access to me, rather than just "more content." Ask me any questions you have.
```
