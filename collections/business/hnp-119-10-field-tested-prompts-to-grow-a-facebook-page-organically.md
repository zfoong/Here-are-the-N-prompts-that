# 10 field-tested prompts to grow a Facebook page organically

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/SimplyDigital.gr/posts/these-are-10-legit-field-tested-prompts-you-can-literally-paste-into-chatgpt-or-/1389240606549728/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Audience Pain Mining Prompt

```text
Act as a Facebook growth strategist. Identify the top 10 emotional pain points, frustrations, and silent struggles of [target audience]. For each pain point, suggest a Facebook post idea that makes the reader feel 'this was written for me' and encourages comments, not likes.
```

### 2. Comment-Bait Without Being Spammy

```text
Create 5 Facebook posts that end with a binary choice or forced opinion question related to [topic]. The question must feel natural, not clickbait, and be easy to answer in under 5 seconds.
```

### 3. Pattern Interrupt Storytelling

```text
Write a short Facebook story (120–180 words) that starts with a controversial or unexpected first line about [topic], then uses storytelling to deliver a lesson, and ends with an open loop that invites readers to share their experience in the comments.
```

### 4. Authority Through Contrarian Takes

```text
List 5 popular beliefs in the [industry/niche]. Rewrite each one as a respectful but bold contrarian Facebook post that challenges the belief and invites discussion without attacking anyone.
```

### 5. Save-Worthy Educational Posts

```text
Create 5 Facebook posts that teach something highly practical about [topic]. Each post must include: – A clear takeaway – Simple steps or bullets – Language that makes people want to save it for later
```

### 6. Relatable Failure Content

```text
Write 3 Facebook posts where the creator admits a mistake or failure related to [topic], explains what went wrong, and asks the audience what they learned from a similar experience.
```

### 7. Scroll-Stopping Hooks Only

```text
Generate 15 Facebook opening lines (first sentence only) about [topic] that are designed to stop scrolling. Avoid hype. Avoid emojis. Make them sound like real thoughts someone would pause to read.
```

### 8. "You're Not Alone" Validation Posts

```text
Create 5 Facebook posts that validate common frustrations people have with [topic]. The tone should feel supportive, honest, and non-judgmental. End each post with a question that encourages people to share their experience.
```

### 9. Micro-Opinion Polls

```text
Create 5 Facebook posts that act like informal polls about [topic]. Each post should ask for a quick opinion (A/B, agree/disagree, yes/no) and encourage people to explain their choice in the comments.
```

### 10. Content Repurposing Engine

```text
Take one core idea about [topic] and repurpose it into: – 1 story-based Facebook post – 1 educational Facebook post – 1 opinion-based Facebook post – 1 short question-only post. Each version must feel native to Facebook, not recycled.
```
