# 5 ChatGPT Prompts for Better Email Marketing

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/MrsMckneely/posts/5-chatgpt-prompts-that-will-take-your-email-marketing-to-the-next-level-/25544426931903145/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Welcome email

```text
Write a warm welcome email for new subscribers who joined my list for tips on [your niche]. Introduce me, explain what they'll learn, and encourage them to reply.
```

### 2. Value email

```text
Write a value-packed email teaching my audience about [topic]. Include 3 helpful tips and end with a soft call to action.
```

### 3. Story email

```text
Write an email that tells a short personal story about how I started [your niche/business] and connect it to a lesson my audience can learn.
```

### 4. Promo email

```text
Write an email promoting my product/service called [product name]. Highlight the problem it solves and include a clear call to action.
```

### 5. Re-engagement email

```text
Write an email to subscribers who haven't opened my emails in a while. Ask if they still want to hear from me and give them a reason to stay.
```
