# Skip the fluff: use these 5 ChatGPT prompts for expert-level email copy

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/skip-the-fluff-use-these-5-chatgpt-prompts-for-expert-level-email-copy-save-this/10173795788145347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The Hook-First Prompt

```text
Write a subject line and first sentence for an email about [insert topic] that immediately grabs attention and creates curiosity—no clickbait.
```

### 2. The Problem-Solution Prompt

```text
Write a short, punchy email that clearly states this problem: [insert pain point] and introduces my product/service as the simplest solution.
```

### 3. The CTA Clarity Prompt

```text
Craft a persuasive email with one single CTA: [insert action]. Keep it conversational, benefit-driven, and no more than 150 words.
```

### 4. The Follow-Up That Doesn't Feel Pushy Prompt

```text
Write a follow-up email that checks in without sounding desperate. Make it friendly, helpful, and offer extra value or info.
```

### 5. The Personalization Layer Prompt

```text
Write a personalized cold outreach email for a [type of lead/client] that references their business, problem, and offers a tailored solution.
```
