# 5 ChatGPT prompts to help you start a side hustle fast

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/ninjaaitools/posts/5-chatgpt-prompts-to-help-you-start-a-side-hustle-fast/2850655391802373/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Find your hustle

```text
I'm a graphic designer with 10 hrs/week. Suggest 10 side hustles I can do and explain how each works.
```

### 2. Test before you waste money

```text
I want to launch a subscription box for eco-friendly pet products. Show me market size, competitors, audience, and pricing.
```

### 3. Steal pro-level copy

```text
Write me an Instagram bio, one-liner pitch, and Etsy product description for custom watercolor portraits.
```

### 4. Become a content machine

```text
I'm a freelance book editor. Give me 12 blog ideas and 12 LinkedIn post ideas to attract authors.
```

### 5. Automate everything

```text
I run a wedding photography side hustle. Build me a weekly schedule that balances shoots, editing, marketing, and family.
```
