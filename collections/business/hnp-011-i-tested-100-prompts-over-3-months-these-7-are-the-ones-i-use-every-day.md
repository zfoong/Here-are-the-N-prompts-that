# 💼 I tested 100+ prompts over 3 months. These 7 are the ones I use every day.

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPT/comments/1r4azlh/i_tested_100_prompts_over_3_months_these_7_are/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Brutal feedback

```text
Critique [IDEA_OR_DRAFT] against [GOAL_AND_CRITERIA]. First list weaknesses and unsupported assumptions, then rank fixes by impact and effort; stay direct but evidence-based.
```

### 2. Learn in layers

```text
Teach [TOPIC] to a smart beginner: three-sentence model, expert nuance, three examples, three misconceptions, and a recommended next topic.
```

### 3. Competitor briefing

```text
Analyze [COMPETITOR] only from [SOURCES]. Cover audience, positioning, content, pricing signals, and three testable gaps; cite evidence and label inference.
```

### 4. One-person marketing team

```text
For [PRODUCT_AUDIENCE_PRICE], create a seven-day campaign, welcome and sales email outlines, three video hooks, and a 30-day calendar tied to [GOAL].
```

### 5. Decision maker

```text
For [DECISION], enumerate options, best/worst/likely outcomes, assumptions, reversibility, and hidden biases; recommend a next step and the missing question.
```

### 6. Content repurposer

```text
Transform [SOURCE_CONTENT] into platform-native versions for [PLATFORMS]. Preserve the core claim, adapt format and tone, and do not invent examples or results.
```

### 7. Second-brain organizer

```text
Organize [ROUGH_NOTES] into themes, actions, priorities, open questions, and connections. Keep original wording for uncertain ideas and produce a compact reference summary.
```
