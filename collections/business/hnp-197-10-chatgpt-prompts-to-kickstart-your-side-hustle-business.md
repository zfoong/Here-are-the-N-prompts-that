# 10 ChatGPT Prompts to Kickstart Your Side Hustle Business

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/isocialyou/posts/10-chatgpt-prompts-to-kickstart-your-side-hustle-business/1258922126274520/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Niche Finder

```text
You are a business strategist. Help me identify a profitable niche for a side hustle in [YOUR INDUSTRY], considering my interests, market demand, and potential competition.
```

### 2. Business Plan Generator

```text
You are an expert business consultant. Help me create a one-page business plan for a side hustle that includes my target audience, value proposition, revenue streams, and marketing plan.
```

### 3. Brand Identity Builder

```text
You are a brand consultant. Write a brand identity statement for my side hustle that defines my mission, vision, and core values, making it clear to customers what I stand for.
```

### 4. Social Media Strategy for Side Hustlers

```text
You are a social media strategist. Create a 3-month social media plan for my side hustle, focusing on organic reach, engagement, and building brand awareness.
```

### 5. Side Hustle Marketing Funnel

```text
You are a marketing expert. Help me create a simple 3-step sales funnel for my side hustle, including the lead magnet, email sequence, and the final conversion strategy.
```

### 6. Quick Start-up Checklist

```text
You are a start-up coach. Provide me with a 10-step checklist to quickly launch my side hustle, from setting up a website to choosing the right tools for success.
```

### 7. Freelance Pitch Template

```text
You are a freelance consultant. Write a cold pitch email template I can send to potential clients for my side hustle, offering my services in [YOUR INDUSTRY].
```

### 8. Customer Feedback System

```text
You are a customer experience expert. Help me create a system for collecting feedback from my first few customers so I can improve my product and build loyalty.
```

### 9. Time Management for Side Hustlers

```text
You are a productivity coach. Create a weekly schedule for someone with a full-time job and a side hustle, ensuring I balance both without burning out.
```

### 10. Launch Announcement

```text
You are a marketing expert. Write a launch announcement for my side hustle that creates excitement and anticipation, encouraging people to check out my new product or service.
```
