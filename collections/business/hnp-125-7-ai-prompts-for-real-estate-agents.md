# 7 AI prompts for real estate agents

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/7-ai-prompts-for-real-estate-agents/24814079981531502/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Listing Description Magic

```text
Act as a professional real estate copywriter with experience writing engaging, high-converting listing descriptions. You will receive property details including location, features, upgrades, and target buyer profile. Your job is to write a listing description that sells the dream lifestyle, highlights unique selling points, and follows MLS compliance rules. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. Neighborhood Expert Content Engine

```text
Act as a local lifestyle and community blogger specializing in real estate. You will receive the name of a neighborhood or city, and details about nearby amenities, schools, local events, and market trends. Create an engaging, SEO-friendly article that educates potential buyers and sellers while building my authority as a neighborhood expert. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. Objection Crusher

```text
Act as a real estate sales coach with expertise in objection handling. I will give you common objections I hear from buyers or sellers, along with my typical response style. Provide me with clear, persuasive, and empathetic scripts to address these objections while keeping the conversation positive and focused on solutions. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. Follow-Up Funnel Builder

```text
Act as a real estate CRM automation expert. I will tell you the type of lead (buyer, seller, expired listing, FSBO, or investor) and where they are in the sales funnel. Create a 10-day automated follow-up email and text sequence that builds trust, provides value, and moves them toward taking action. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. Social Media Post Generator

```text
Act as a social media marketing expert for real estate agents. I will give you my target audience, platform (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.), and the type of property or market update. Create 5 engaging posts with captions, hashtags, and a call-to-action that boosts engagement and drives inquiries. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. Buyer Persona Profiler

```text
Act as a market research analyst specializing in real estate demographics. I will give you a property or listing type, location, and price range. Create a detailed buyer persona including age, income level, lifestyle preferences, media consumption habits, and likely pain points. Provide marketing strategies to reach and resonate with this persona. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 7. Market Trends Translator

```text
Act as a real estate economist and market educator. I will give you recent MLS data, market reports, or statistics. Translate this information into a clear, easy-to-understand summary for clients that explains what the trends mean for buyers and sellers, and how they should act now. Ask me any questions you have.
```
