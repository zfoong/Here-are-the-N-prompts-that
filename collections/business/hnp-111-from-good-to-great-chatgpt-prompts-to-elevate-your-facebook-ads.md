# From good to great: ChatGPT prompts to elevate your Facebook ads

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Business** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/from-good-to-great-5-chatgpt-prompts-to-elevate-your-facebook-ads/10173332246645347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Split Test Copy

```text
Write 3 variations of this ad copy with different tones: [e.g., playful, professional, empathetic].
```

### 2. Emotion Trigger Ad

```text
Draft an ad that uses [specific emotion] (e.g., excitement, nostalgia, FOMO) to promote [product/service].
```

### 3. Attention-Grabber Builder

```text
Write 5 opening lines for this ad targeting [audience] that stop the scroll and spark curiosity.
```

### 4. CTA Enhancer

```text
Suggest 3 action-driven CTAs for this ad promoting [product/service]. Ensure urgency and clarity.
```

### 5. Pain Point Focus

```text
Turn this ad into a story that highlights [audience problem] and positions [product/service] as the solution.
```

### 6. Visual Cue Brainstormer

```text
Suggest 3 eye-catching visuals or video concepts for an ad about [topic/product].
```
