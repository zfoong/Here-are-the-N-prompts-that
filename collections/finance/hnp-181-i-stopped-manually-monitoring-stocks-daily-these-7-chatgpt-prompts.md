# I stopped manually monitoring stocks daily: these 7 ChatGPT prompts

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Finance** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/100089314351644/posts/i-stopped-manually-monitoring-stocks-daily-these-7-chatgpt-prompts-now-track-eva/799276789726126/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Portfolio Balance Analysis

```text
Here's my current portfolio: [list tickers + allocation]. Give me a straightforward analysis of how balanced/diversified it is, and highlight any overconcentrated sectors or risky holdings.
```

### 2. Daily Market News Summary

```text
Summarize the top stock market news today in no more than 4 bullet points, and explain how they could affect tech, energy, or index funds.
```

### 3. Undervalued Stock Screener

```text
Provide me a list of undervalued stocks below $55 with solid fundamentals, including P/E ratio, dividend yield, and growth potential.
```

### 4. Investment Style Check

```text
Based on my goal of [growth/income/stability] and timeframe of [X years], how aggressive or conservative is my current investment style?
```

### 5. Long-Term Watchlist Builder

```text
Recommend 6 stocks worth adding to a long-term watchlist for steady growth over the next 6–12 years. Include basic reasons and trends behind each choice.
```

### 6. Earnings Report Explainer

```text
Explain [insert stock's] latest earnings report in simple terms. What stood out, and what might it indicate for future results?
```

### 7. Google Sheets Stock Tracker

```text
Assist me in creating a simple Google Sheets stock tracker that pulls live prices and highlights daily/weekly changes. Include columns for cost basis, % change, and target price.
```
