# 7 ChatGPT prompts to fix your finances

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Finance** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/Owwstin/posts/i-gave-chatgpt-access-to-my-finances-and-it-fixed-my-entire-financial-lifethese-/10160670424096256/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Find money leaks

```text
Here's my income, bills, and debts. Where am I losing the most money?
```

### 2. 50/30/20 budget

```text
Can you design a 50/30/20 budget that actually works for how I live?
```

### 3. Hidden subscriptions

```text
What tiny purchases and subscriptions are adding up without me realizing?
```

### 4. Low-risk digital product

```text
How do I use AI to create a low-risk digital product I can start selling fast?
```

### 5. Surprise income plan

```text
What should I do with surprise income like tax refunds or side gig money to prepare for my future?
```

### 6. Debt payoff plan

```text
Make me a debt payoff plan that's fast but won't burn me out.
```

### 7. Automate saving

```text
What are 3 ways to automate saving money with all of my streams of income?
```
