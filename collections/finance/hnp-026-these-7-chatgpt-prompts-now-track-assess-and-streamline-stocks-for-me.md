# 💸 These 7 ChatGPT prompts now track, assess, and streamline stocks for me

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Finance** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/threalmelaninking/posts/i-stopped-manually-monitoring-stocks-dailythese-7-chatgpt-prompts-now-track-asse/757972823754420/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Portfolio concentration review

```text
Using [HOLDINGS_ALLOCATIONS] and a current trusted classification source, calculate concentration by asset, sector, geography, and currency; state missing data and do not recommend trades.
```

### 2. Daily market brief

```text
Summarize today's market-moving events from [TRUSTED_SOURCES] with timestamps, affected sectors, alternative interpretations, and links; distinguish fact from market speculation.
```

### 3. Stock screen specification

```text
Design—not execute—a screen for [OBJECTIVE] using valuation, quality, liquidity, and risk criteria. Explain data definitions, survivorship bias, and where current licensed data is required.
```

### 4. Risk-profile comparison

```text
Compare [PORTFOLIO] with my stated [GOAL_TIME_HORIZON_RISK_CAPACITY]. Identify mismatches and questions for a fiduciary; do not infer tolerance from returns alone.
```

### 5. Watchlist research plan

```text
Create a research watchlist process for [COMPANIES_OR_THEME] with thesis, disconfirming evidence, filing dates, valuation inputs, and review triggers; do not predict returns.
```

### 6. Earnings explainer

```text
Summarize [COMPANY]'s latest official earnings materials in plain language, citing period and sources. Separate reported figures, management guidance, and analyst inference.
```

### 7. Tracker design

```text
Design a spreadsheet schema for holdings, cost basis, price source, return, allocation, and review date. Explain safe data connections and formula checks without pretending live access.
```
