# 7 prompts to figure out when and how you can retire

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Finance** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/100089314351644/posts/breaking-chatgpt-can-now-map-out-a-retirement-plan-better-than-most-expensive-pl/960866960233774/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Retirement Number Calculator – Practical Version

```text
Calculate my actual retirement number using current lifestyle [$X/month], inflation [X%], life expectancy [age Y], current savings/investments [paste], and expected income sources. Target a sustainable success rate of 80–90%.
```

### 2. Withdrawal Strategy Stress Test

```text
Test my portfolio under different withdrawal strategies: 4%, 3.5%, 3%, variable rates, and guardrails. Show safe withdrawal amounts in today's value and highlight sequence-of-returns risk. Details: [portfolio size, allocation, timeline].
```

### 3. Retirement Income Timing Guide

```text
Based on my birth year [XXXX], earnings history [paste], family situation, and health outlook, suggest the best age to start benefits. Compare options like early, full retirement age, or delayed. Show breakeven points and lifetime totals.
```

### 4. Tax-Efficient Conversion Plan

```text
Create a multi-year strategy for converting traditional retirement funds into tax-advantaged accounts. Include current balances [paste], tax bracket today vs retirement, and required distribution considerations.
```

### 5. Pre-Retirement Healthcare Plan

```text
Build a year-by-year healthcare funding strategy before age 65. Include insurance options, subsidy strategies, savings usage, and estimated costs. My age: [X], income plan: [paste].
```

### 6. Portfolio Strategy by Retirement Phase

```text
Recommend an asset allocation plan from [X] years before retirement, during retirement, and 15–25 years after. Include allocations for cash, bonds, equities, and inflation protection, plus when to adjust risk levels.
```

### 7. Retirement Readiness Scorecard

```text
Evaluate my readiness for retiring at [target age] across key areas (savings rate, net worth, withdrawal strategy, healthcare, taxes, income streams). Current stats: [paste]. Identify the most important gaps to fix first.
```
