# My financial advisor got nervous after I started using these 5 ChatGPT prompts

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Finance** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/Owwstin/posts/my-financial-advisor-got-nervous-after-i-started-using-these-5-chatgpt-prompts-/10160832864306256/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Personalized Budget Creator

```text
Create a clear, achievable monthly budget for me based on my income and spending habits [paste details].
```

### 2. Automated Savings Plan

```text
Suggest the best savings plan to build an emergency fund of 10K within one year.
```

### 3. Investment Basics

```text
Explain 3 beginner-friendly investment options clearly, and recommend exactly how much I should invest.
```

### 4. Side Hustle Finder

```text
List 5 profitable side hustle ideas based on my skills [list your skills]. Include exactly how to start.
```

### 5. Debt Repayment Strategy

```text
Give me a step-by-step plan to pay off 5K in credit card debt in less than 6 months without drastically changing my lifestyle.
```
