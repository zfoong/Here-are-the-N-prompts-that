# After payday, do this: 6 ChatGPT prompts to build a personal money system that actually works

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Finance** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/-after-payday-do-this-6-chatgpt-prompts-to-build-a-personal-money-system-that-ac/10173587858345347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Create a 50/30/20 Budget Based on My Paycheck

```text
I earn [insert income] per month. Break this down using the 50/30/20 budgeting method and allocate amounts for needs, wants, and savings/debt.
```

### 2. Design a Payday-to-Payday Money Flow Plan

```text
I get paid every [insert schedule]. Build me a financial system from one payday to the next—covering bills, essentials, savings, and guilt-free spending.
```

### 3. Identify Where I'm Overspending and How to Fix It

```text
Here are my monthly expenses: [insert]. Analyze these and show me where I can cut back without feeling restricted.
```

### 4. Automate My Savings Based on My Income

```text
Help me build a savings automation system with small transfers that add up—using percentages from my paycheck or fixed amounts.
```

### 5. Create a Mini-Investing Plan That Fits My Budget

```text
Assume I have $[insert] left after bills and savings. How can I start investing safely with this amount each month, even if it's small?
```

### 6. Write a Simple Debt Payoff Strategy With My Current Income

```text
I owe [insert debt details]. With my income of [insert amount], give me a debt repayment strategy that balances payoff speed and financial breathing room.
```
