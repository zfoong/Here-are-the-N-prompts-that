# I organized 150 ChatGPT prompts specifically for freelance writers — here are 10 of the best ones free

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content Creation** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPT/comments/1svq5zs/i_organized_150_chatgpt_prompts_specifically_for)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. When you're blocked

```text
I need to write [piece type] about [topic] for [audience]. Give me 5 opening sentences that are structurally different — one scene, one statistic, one direct question, one counterintuitive claim, one under 8 words. Don't explain them.
```

### 2. Bad briefs

```text
My client sent me this brief: [paste]. It's vague in these ways: [describe]. Rewrite it as a clear brief I can work from and flag the 3 decisions I still need the client to make.
```

### 3. Titles

```text
I need a title for a piece about [topic] for [publication]. Give me 8 options. No colons. No question marks. Each must work as a standalone statement.
```

### 4. Editing rhythm

```text
Edit this paragraph for rhythm: [paste]. Goal is variation — short sentences next to long ones. Show the edit and mark what changed.
```

### 5. Finding the real lede

```text
My editor says this piece buries the lede. Read it and tell me where the real lede is: [paste].
```

### 6. Content repurposing

```text
Take this piece: [paste]. Extract 5 social posts — one LinkedIn, one Twitter thread opener, one Instagram caption, one newsletter blurb, one Substack note. Each should feel native to its platform.
```

### 7. Pushing back on feedback

```text
A client gave me this feedback: [paste]. It's unclear. Give me 3 specific clarifying questions I can send back.
```

### 8. Rate conversations

```text
I charge [rate] for [service]. Write 3 ways to explain this price to a client who thinks it's expensive — without discounting and without sounding defensive. Each under 40 words.
```

### 9. Weak endings

```text
My ending is weak. Read this draft: [paste]. Give me 3 alternatives — one that callbacks to the opening, one that zooms out, one that ends on action or image.
```

### 10. Positioning

```text
I specialize in [niche]. Write a one-paragraph positioning statement for my website that's specific enough to attract the right clients and repel the wrong ones.
```
