# 8 AI prompts for amazing Instagram Reels

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content Creation** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/8-ai-prompts-for-amazing-instagram-reels/26134553682817452/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The "Saveable" Mini-Tutorial

```text
Act as an Instagram Growth Strategist. [Context: I want to create a high-value "mini-tutorial" for my business, [Insert Business Name]. My goal is to get viewers to "Save" the post so they can refer back to it later.] [Task: Outline a 30-second Reel script that teaches [Insert Specific Skill/Tip] in 3 clear steps. For each step, describe the on-screen text that should appear. Ensure the caption ends with a reason for them to save it, like: "Save this so you don't forget these steps later!"] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. The "B-Roll + Voiceover" Connection

```text
Act as a Creative Content Producer. [Context: I am hesitant to be on camera. I want to show a "Day in the Life" of my business [Insert Business Type] using only B-roll footage of my desk, my products, or my process.] [Task: Create a 5-shot list of B-roll clips I can film (3 seconds each). Then, write a 20-second warm, authentic voiceover script where I explain the "why" behind my business. Suggest a type of trending audio to play at low volume (10-15%) under my voice.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. The "Before & After" Transformation

```text
Act as a Direct-Response Marketer. [Context: I want to show the results of [Insert Product/Service]. I have a "before" state of [Insert Starting Point] and an "after" state of [Insert Result].] [Task: Write a script for a 15-second Reel that starts with the "After" shot for 2 seconds (the Hook). Then, use a quick transition to show the "Before" and a 5-second montage of the work. End with a "Link in Bio" call to action.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. The "Myth-Buster" Text Overlay

```text
Act as an Industry Authority. [Context: In my niche, [Insert Niche], people often wrongly believe [Insert Common Myth]. I want to debunk this using a short Reel.] [Task: Create a script for a 10-second Reel. Describe a simple movement I can do (like pointing to invisible text) and provide the exact 3 lines of on-screen text that will appear to debunk the myth. Include a caption that invites "Conversation Depth" by asking: "Did you used to believe this too? Let me know below."] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. The 2026 "Conversation Depth" Caption

```text
Act as a Social Media Engagement Expert. [Context: I have a video of [Insert Video Description]. I want the caption to do the heavy lifting of starting a real conversation.] [Task: Write a 3-paragraph caption for this Reel. Paragraph 1 should be a relatable story. Paragraph 2 should provide a "Deep Insight." Paragraph 3 must be a "Better Question" that requires more than a one-word answer to help boost my "Social Relevance" score.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. The "Teleprompter-Ready" FAQ

```text
Act as a Customer Success Manager. [Context: I want to answer the 3 most common questions I get about [Insert Product/Service] in one Reel.] [Task: Write 3 short "scripts" (max 10 seconds each) answering these questions. Format them for a teleprompter so they are easy to read. Suggest where I should add a "Zoom-in" effect in the editing to keep the pacing tight.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 7. The SEO-Optimized Reel Cover

```text
Act as an Instagram SEO Specialist. [Context: I am posting a Reel about [Insert Topic]. My target keywords are [Insert 2-3 Keywords].] [Task: Suggest 3 different text-heavy Reel cover designs. Each should clearly state the "Benefit" of watching. Then, provide 3 broad and 3 niche hashtags that align with the 2026 algorithm's preference for topic clusters.] Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 8. The "Looping" Value Stack

```text
Act as a High-Retention Content Creator. [Context: I want to share a list of [Insert Number, e.g., 5] tools/tips for [Insert Audience]. I want the video to be very fast-paced so people have to watch it twice to read everything.] [Task: Create a script for a 15-second "Value Stack." Provide the text for 5 fast-flashing text overlays. Write a "Looping" script where the final sentence connects perfectly to the start of the video to trick the viewer into a second loop.] Ask me any questions you have.
```
