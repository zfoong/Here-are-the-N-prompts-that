# 7 prompts to make ChatGPT write like a human

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content Creation** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/Owwstin/posts/stop-telling-chatgpt-to-write-like-a-human-telling-it-to-remove-an-em-dash-isnt-/10161043453636256/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. TikTok doomscroll rewrite

```text
Rewrite this for someone doomscrolling TikTok: short, sharp, and addictive.
```

### 2. Viral tweet tone

```text
Make this read like a tweet that goes viral — every word should slap.
```

### 3. Direct-response marketer tone

```text
Give this the tone of a direct-response marketer who's done $10M in sales.
```

### 4. Strip the fluff

```text
Strip the fluff. Make every sentence fight for its life.
```

### 5. Inject personality

```text
Inject personality: sarcastic, witty, and just bold enough to trigger someone.
```

### 6. Texting a skeptical friend

```text
Pretend you're texting this to your most skeptical friend.
```

### 7. Value-packed thread

```text
Turn this into a value-packed thread people actually want to save.
```
