# 5 AI Prompts for Creating a Week of Content in 90 Minutes

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content Creation** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/Entrepreneurs/comments/1tz8rvo)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Hook Builder

```text
You are a content strategist for [your niche]. My audience is [describe them in 2 sentences]. Their #1 frustration is [X]. Write 10 scroll-stopping hooks for a post about [topic]. Each hook should create curiosity or speak directly to that frustration. Format: one hook per line, no explanation.
```

### 2. Caption Expander

```text
Take this hook: [paste hook]. Expand it into a [platform] caption that: opens with the hook, delivers one actionable insight in 3 sentences, and closes with [your CTA]. My tone is [casual/professional/etc]. Max [X] words.
```

### 3. Repurpose Machine

```text
I have this long-form content: [paste]. Extract 5 standalone social posts from it. Each should work as a standalone piece — no 'as I said above' references. Formats: 2 short tweets, 1 Instagram caption, 1 LinkedIn post, 1 TikTok hook.
```

### 4. Title Generator (YouTube)

```text
My video is about [topic]. My channel niche is [niche]. Target viewer: [description]. Write 10 YouTube title options. Mix these angles: search-intent titles, curiosity-gap titles, results-based titles. Each must be under 60 characters.
```

### 5. Weekly Content Calendar

```text
I create content about [topic] for [audience]. Suggest 7 content ideas for this week — one per day. For each: the angle, the platform best suited, the emotional hook, and the core message in one sentence. Avoid generic advice.
```
