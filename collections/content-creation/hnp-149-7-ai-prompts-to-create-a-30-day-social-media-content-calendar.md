# 7 AI prompts to create a 30-day social media content calendar

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content Creation** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/7-ai-prompts-to-create-a-30-day-social-media-content-calendar-/24766723959600438/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Your Ultimate 30-Day Calendar Blueprint

```text
Act as an experienced social media strategist. I'm an entrepreneur launching a 30-day social media campaign. I need a content calendar that adapts to my specified number of posts per day and platform mix (e.g., Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter). Please create a table outlining each day's theme, post type, caption idea, and suggested posting times for my variables. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. Dynamic Platform-Specific Planner

```text
Act as a multi-platform social media consultant. I run a small business and want a 30-day calendar that adjusts based on how many posts I want per day and which platforms (e.g., Facebook, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn Articles). Provide a daily schedule with content themes, recommended formats, and platform-specific tips. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. Engagement-Boosting Content Themes

```text
Act as a content marketing expert. I need a 30-day social media calendar that I can customize for X posts per day across Y platforms. Include daily themes designed to boost engagement, such as polls, tips, user stories, and product highlights. Present it in a clear, editable format. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. Hashtag and Caption Masterplan

```text
Act as a social media copywriting coach. Create a 30-day calendar for me, with variables for number of posts per day and platform selection. For each slot, suggest a headline, 3–5 relevant hashtags, and a caption hook. Include notes on best posting times and any platform nuances. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. Visual Content Scheduling Guide

```text
Act as a visual content strategist. I want a 30-day calendar with adjustable posts per day and platforms. For each day, specify whether it's an image, carousel, video, or story, plus a brief creative brief. Format as a spreadsheet-ready list. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. Analytics-Driven Posting Plan

```text
Act as a data-driven social media analyst. Help me build a 30-day calendar that adapts to how many posts I choose per day and the platforms I select. For each post, recommend posting times based on engagement stats and explain why. Provide a summary of key metrics to track. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 7. Content Repurposing Strategy

```text
Act as a content repurposing expert. I need a 30-day social media calendar that accounts for X posts per day across multiple platforms. Identify core content pieces and show how to adapt them (e.g., blog ➔ tweet thread ➔ Instagram carousel). Include a schedule and repurposing notes. Ask me any questions you have.
```
