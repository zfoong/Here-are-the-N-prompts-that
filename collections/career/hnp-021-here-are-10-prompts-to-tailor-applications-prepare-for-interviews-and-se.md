# 🧭 Here are 10 prompts to tailor applications, prepare for interviews, and secure a role

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Career** · [Original post ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/heynayeem_copy-and-paste-these-chatgpt-prompts-to-land-activity-7263183269284720640-e7J2)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Tailor a resume

```text
Tailor [RESUME] to [JOB_DESCRIPTION] for [ROLE]. Map existing evidence to requirements, improve wording, and list gaps; never invent experience.
```

### 2. Cover letter

```text
Draft a cover letter for [ROLE] at [COMPANY] using [EXPERIENCE] and [COMPANY_EVIDENCE]. Connect two proven strengths to role needs without flattery or fabricated values.
```

### 3. LinkedIn profile optimizer

```text
Audit [PROFILE] for recruiter discovery in [INDUSTRY]. Suggest evidence-based headline, about, and skills edits while preserving my actual history.
```

### 4. Common interview questions

```text
Generate likely interview questions for [ROLE], explain what each tests, and help me build truthful answer outlines from [EXPERIENCE].
```

### 5. Behavioral answer builder

```text
Turn [REAL_EXPERIENCE] into a STAR answer for [QUESTION]. Ask for missing context, emphasize my decisions, and never add results.
```

### 6. Company research brief

```text
Using current cited sources, summarize [COMPANY] mission, products, recent developments, and culture signals; distinguish official claims from outside reporting.
```

### 7. Salary negotiation prep

```text
Using current cited compensation data for [ROLE_LOCATION_LEVEL], propose a range, rationale, questions, and a respectful negotiation script; flag data limits.
```

### 8. Mock interview

```text
Interview me for [ROLE] with five situational or technical questions one at a time, then score answers against a stated rubric and suggest specific practice.
```

### 9. Job-description analyzer

```text
Analyze [JOB_DESCRIPTION] for core outcomes, required evidence, keywords, ambiguities, and likely screening criteria; show what my application should substantiate.
```

### 10. Networking outreach

```text
Draft a short message to [PERSON] at [COMPANY] based on [REAL_CONNECTION_OR_INTEREST], asking one informed question without requesting a job or pretending familiarity.
```
