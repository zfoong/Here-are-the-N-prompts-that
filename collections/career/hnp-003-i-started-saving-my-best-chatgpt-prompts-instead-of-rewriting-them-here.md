# 🧭 I started saving my best ChatGPT prompts instead of rewriting them. Here are 5 I actually reuse.

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Career** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPT/comments/1ts2b3y/i_started_saving_my_best_chatgpt_prompts_instead/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Essay outliner

```text
Build an argument outline for [QUESTION] using my thesis [THESIS] and notes [NOTES]. Include claims, evidence needed, counterarguments, and a logical section order; do not write the essay.
```

### 2. Resume feedback

```text
Review my resume [RESUME] against [JOB_DESCRIPTION]. Separate missing evidence, unclear wording, and formatting issues; recommend edits without inventing experience.
```

### 3. Side-hustle ideas

```text
Suggest side hustles that fit [SKILLS], [TIME], [BUDGET], and [CONSTRAINTS]. Explain the customer, offer, first validation step, and main risk for each.
```

### 4. Cold email

```text
Draft a cold email to [RECIPIENT] about [RELEVANT_PROBLEM]. Lead with a specific observation, offer one useful outcome, stay under [WORD_LIMIT] words, and end with a low-friction question.
```

### 5. Difficult conversation

```text
Help me prepare for a conversation with [PERSON] about [ISSUE]. Separate facts from interpretations, propose an opening, questions to ask, boundaries, and de-escalation options.
```
