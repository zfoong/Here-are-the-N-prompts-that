# 🧭 My friend applied to 57 jobs and was ghosted. Here are the 7 prompts we used.

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Career** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/Owwstin/posts/my-friend-applied-to-57-jobsghosted-57-timesnot-a-single-callthen-i-gave-chatgpt/10160923539396256/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Recruiter gap audit

```text
Act as a recruiter for [INDUSTRY] and compare [RESUME] with [JOB_DESCRIPTION]. Identify missing evidence or clarity that could block a screen; do not invent qualifications.
```

### 2. Targeted summary

```text
Rewrite my resume summary for [JOB_DESCRIPTION] using only facts in [RESUME]. Keep it concise, role-specific, and free of unsupported adjectives.
```

### 3. Achievement bullets

```text
Turn [BULLETS] into action-and-result statements. Ask for metrics when absent and leave placeholders rather than fabricating numbers.
```

### 4. Career-gap framing

```text
Help me truthfully explain [CAREER_GAP] using [ACTIVITIES_AND_CONTEXT], focusing on relevant growth without hiding dates or inventing work.
```

### 5. ATS keyword alignment

```text
Extract genuine skills and terms from [JOB_DESCRIPTION], map them to evidence in [RESUME], and recommend natural wording only where the match is true.
```

### 6. Readable resume format

```text
Recommend a simple, scannable resume structure for [ROLE_AND_COUNTRY], including headings, order, length, and accessibility; avoid claiming universal ATS compatibility.
```

### 7. Hiring-manager message

```text
Draft a brief message to [HIRING_MANAGER] based on [REAL_RELEVANCE], one evidenced contribution, and a low-pressure question. Avoid desperation and false familiarity.
```
