# 10 powerful ChatGPT prompts for career growth

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Career** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/puttingchicagotowork/posts/10-powerful-chatgpt-prompts-for-career-growth1-resume-enhancement-optimize-your-/1097851435709926/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Resume Enhancement

```text
Optimize your resume for a [job title] position at [company] to pass ATS and capture recruiters' attention.
```

### 2. Tailored Cover Letter

```text
Craft a persuasive cover letter that aligns with [company]'s values and highlights your fit for a [job title] role.
```

### 3. Interview Preparation

```text
Access the top 20 interview questions and sample answers tailored for a [job title] position.
```

### 4. Salary Negotiation

```text
Develop a winning salary negotiation strategy based on industry benchmarks for a [job title] role.
```

### 5. LinkedIn Profile Optimization

```text
Improve your LinkedIn profile with strategic keywords and accomplishments to attract job opportunities.
```

### 6. Effective Networking

```text
Write compelling LinkedIn connection requests and messages to engage industry professionals.
```

### 7. Personal Branding

```text
Build authority in your niche with content strategies and positioning tactics.
```

### 8. Mock Interview Practice

```text
Simulate an interview and receive real-time feedback to refine your responses.
```

### 9. Career Change Roadmap

```text
Get a step-by-step guide for transitioning into a new industry with confidence.
```

### 10. Strategic Job Search

```text
Design a customized job search strategy to land your ideal role efficiently.
```
