# ✍️ Here are the 5 prompts I use that actually work

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/zubinkutar_top-5-chat-gpt-prompts-to-create-viral-linkedin-activity-7382262820135976961-LYbm)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Trend rider

```text
Using current cited trends in [FIELD], propose five LinkedIn ideas with an emotional hook, defensible opinion, and personal angle I can support with [EXPERIENCE].
```

### 2. Storyteller

```text
Turn [IDEA_AND_FACTS] into a LinkedIn outline with opening, narrative movement, and reflective takeaway. Do not invent personal details.
```

### 3. Voice finder

```text
Rewrite [POST_IDEA] in bold, empathetic, and curious tones. Preserve the same facts and explain which audience expectation each tone serves.
```

### 4. Hook master

```text
Compress [POST] into five three-line hook options that create clarity or tension without clickbait or unsupported promises.
```

### 5. Conversation starter

```text
Write three good-faith closing questions for [POST] that invite experience or disagreement rather than farming one-word comments.
```
