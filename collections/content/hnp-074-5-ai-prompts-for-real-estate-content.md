# 5 AI prompts that write real estate content in seconds — listing descriptions, emails, social posts and more

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/Entrepreneurs/comments/1t3fpca/5_ai_prompts_that_write_real_estate_content_in)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. MLS Listing Description

```text
Write a compelling listing description for [BEDROOMS]-bed, [BATHROOMS]-bath home in [NEIGHBORHOOD]. Features: [LIST]. Price: [$PRICE]. Target buyer: [BUYER TYPE]. Tone: warm, vivid.
```

### 2. Social Media Caption

```text
Write 5 Instagram captions for new listing at [ADDRESS] priced at [$PRICE]. Features: [LIST]. Mix: storytelling, question-based, urgency. Include relevant hashtags for [CITY].
```

### 3. Lead Follow-Up Text

```text
Write a follow-up text to lead [NAME] who asked about [PROPERTY TYPE] in [AREA]. Under 50 words. Warm not pushy. Goal: start a conversation.
```

### 4. Open House Email

```text
Write an open house invitation email for [ADDRESS] on [DATE] at [TIME]. Include features, price, parking, and a personal note about the property.
```

### 5. Negotiation Talking Points

```text
Write talking points for presenting a low offer of [$OFFER] vs list price [$LIST] to my seller client. Frame it constructively. Guide them toward a strategic counter.
```
