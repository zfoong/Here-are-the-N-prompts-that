# 9 AI prompts for culturally relevant content production

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/9-ai-prompts-for-culturally-relevant-content-production/25943038061969016/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Content Blueprint and Cultural Vetting

```text
Act as a Cross-Cultural Curriculum Designer and Research Analyst. My core idea is [Summarize Core Idea/Topic, e.g., "Integrating Restorative Justice Practices into Middle School Discipline"]. The target audience for this educational package is [Describe Audience, e.g., "Educators in urban schools serving a high percentage of Latinx students"]. The final content must be culturally relevant and responsive to [Identify Specific Cultural Norms/Values to Include, e.g., emphasis on community, family involvement]. Design the table of contents for a comprehensive 10-section Full Report. Title the first section "[Section Title: Culturally Responsive Foundation]" and detail the required content for this foundational section, ensuring it explicitly references the specific cultural norms/values you identified. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. Report Narrative and Key Findings Writer

```text
Act as an Academic Writer and Policy Expert specializing in evidence-based program implementation. Using the core idea [The Core Idea/Topic], write the "Key Findings" and "Recommendations" section for the full report. The findings must be structured around [Insert Number, e.g., 4] major bullet points. Draft a 250-word narrative that summarizes these findings, emphasizing the positive impact on [Insert Target Metric, e.g., student engagement, staff retention] within the context of [Identify Specific Cultural Context/Challenge]. The recommendations must be practical and implementation-focused. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. Matching Presentation Slide Outline

```text
Act as an Executive Presentation Designer and Adult Learning Facilitator. I need to convert the Full Report (which addresses [The Core Idea/Topic]) into a matching 45-minute professional presentation. The audience is [Describe Audience, e.g., "School District Administrators"] and the goal is to secure buy-in for funding. Create a slide-by-slide outline (15 slides maximum). For the first 5 slides, generate the main slide title and a concise, bulleted description of the image/visual concept that uses imagery relevant to [Identify Specific Cultural Imagery to use, e.g., collaborative circles, intergenerational learning]. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. Culturally Relevant Icebreaker and Hook

```text
Act as an expert in Culturally Responsive Pedagogy and Workshop Facilitation. I need a 5-minute interactive activity to open the presentation on [The Core Idea/Topic]. The hook must resonate with the target audience [Describe Audience] by appealing to their sense of [Identify Core Cultural Value, e.g., collective responsibility]. Design a brief, low-risk, small-group icebreaker exercise. Provide the exact text for the instruction slide and two specific open-ended discussion prompts that link their personal/cultural experience to the presentation topic. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. Workbook Activity and Reflection Designer

```text
Act as a Curriculum Workbook Creator and Reflective Practice Specialist. I need to design a practical, hands-on activity for the accompanying Workbook that reinforces the concept of [Identify Key Concept from the Report]. The activity must be culturally relevant by asking the user to reflect on an experience within their [Specify Cultural/Community Context, e.g., family or religious institution]. Design a two-page workbook spread. Title the activity and provide the step-by-step instructions. Include a "Reflect & Connect" journaling box with three probing questions related to the activity. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. Glossary of Terms and Cultural Context

```text
Act as a Technical Writer and Cultural Liaison. Generate a glossary entry for the following three terms from the report: [Term 1], [Term 2], and [Term 3]. For each term, provide a clear, one-sentence definition. Then, for the term [Select One Term to Contextualize], write a short, 3-sentence annotation that explains how this term's application might be uniquely understood or interpreted within the culture of [Identify Target Cultural Group]. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 7. Action Planning Template Creator

```text
Act as a Strategic Planning Consultant. The final section of the workbook needs an "Implementation Action Plan" template for users to apply the report's recommendations. The plan must be segmented to account for varying resources. Create a three-column table template titled "Three-Tiered Implementation Plan." Label the columns: 1. Low Effort/High Impact Action (for limited time/budget), 2. Mid-Level/Collaborative Action, and 3. Long-Term/Systemic Change. Provide a concrete example of an action item for the topic [Insert Key Action Area from Report] in each of the three columns. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 8. Report Title and Branding Refinement

```text
Act as a Brand Naming Specialist and Educational Marketing Consultant. I need a powerful, professional title and subtitle for the entire integrated project (Report, Presentation, and Workbook). The current working title is [Insert Current Working Title]. Generate 5 new, compelling title and subtitle combinations. Each combination must include a high-value action verb (e.g., Transforming, Unlocking, Systematizing) and clearly reference the cultural focus and the target metric: [Insert Target Metric]. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 9. Inter-Document Cross-Reference Checker

```text
Act as a Quality Assurance Editor and Cross-Referencing Expert. I need a simple internal checklist to ensure the three documents—Report, Presentation, and Workbook—are perfectly aligned. Create a three-point checklist to verify structural alignment across the three documents. The checks must confirm that [Check 1: Section/Chapter Titles Match], [Check 2: Key Vocabulary is Consistent], and [Check 3: The Call-to-Action/Next Step is Identical] across all three final outputs.
```
