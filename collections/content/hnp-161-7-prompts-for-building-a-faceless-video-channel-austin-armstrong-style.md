# 7 prompts for building a faceless video channel (Austin Armstrong style)

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/7-prompts-for-building-a-faceless-video-channel-austin-armstrong-style/24861018356837664/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Faceless Content Strategy Blueprint

```text
Act as a viral short-form content strategist specializing in faceless video creation, inspired by Austin Armstrong's techniques. I run a [insert niche: e.g., finance, fitness, AI tools, etc.] channel and want to grow it past one million views. Create a step-by-step 90-day faceless content plan using Syllaby, including video themes, posting frequency, and monetization ideas. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. 10 Viral Hook Ideas for Faceless Videos

```text
Act as a viral content copywriter who studies Austin Armstrong's short-form hooks. I need 10 faceless video hooks designed to grab attention in the first 3 seconds for my [insert niche] channel. Make sure the hooks are curiosity-driven and formatted for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. Faceless Script Writing Assistant

```text
Act as a faceless video scriptwriter inspired by Austin Armstrong's proven style. My channel is about [insert topic]. Write 5 engaging 30-second faceless video scripts optimized for Syllaby that keep viewers watching until the end. Make them easy to narrate over stock footage, text overlays, or AI avatars. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. AI Tools & Faceless Editing Workflow

```text
Act as a faceless video production expert who uses Syllaby to automate editing. Build me a step-by-step workflow to produce and publish 5 faceless videos per week in under 3 hours of work. Include tools, templates, and automation tips. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. Trending Sound & Caption Optimization

```text
Act as a short-form algorithm expert who studies trending audio and caption strategies. For my [insert niche] faceless channel, recommend 10 trending sounds and give me optimized captions and hashtags to maximize reach and virality. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. Monetization Path to $10k/Month

```text
Act as a faceless content monetization strategist who specializes in leveraging Syllaby for faceless video creation. Assume my channel is on pace to cross 1M views. Create a roadmap for monetizing via ads, affiliate marketing, and digital products. Show me exactly how to build faceless content that generates $10k/month. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 7. Analytics Growth Hacking

```text
Act as a short-form analytics growth hacker like Austin Armstrong. Teach me how to study analytics (watch time, CTR, retention) and adjust my faceless content strategy weekly to double my growth speed. Give me a repeatable checklist. Ask me any questions you have.
```
