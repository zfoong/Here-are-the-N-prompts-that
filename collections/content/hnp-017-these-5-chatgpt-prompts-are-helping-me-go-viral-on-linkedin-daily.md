# ✍️ These 5 ChatGPT prompts are helping me go viral on LinkedIn daily

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/austinarmstrong90_everyone-thinks-growing-on-linkedin-is-hard-activity-7472988322659811328-aLXa)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Viral hook generator

```text
From [IDEA] and [EVIDENCE], write 15 LinkedIn first lines under ten words using curiosity, contrast, or a specific number; no clichés or unsupported promises.
```

### 2. Audience decoder

```text
For audience [AUDIENCE], hypothesize ten questions, five questionable beliefs, and three desired outcomes. Label these as hypotheses and propose research to verify them.
```

### 3. Story-to-post translator

```text
Turn [REAL_STORY] into a LinkedIn draft with one-line hook, four short paragraphs, and a closing lesson. Preserve uncertainty and keep it personal, not promotional.
```

### 4. Comment magnet

```text
Propose ten debate-worthy ideas in [NICHE], each with a defensible opinion, concrete example, and good-faith question; avoid outrage bait.
```

### 5. Thirty-day content engine

```text
Create a 30-day plan for [NICHE], [AUDIENCE], and [GOAL], balancing stories, expertise, proof, and conversation; include inputs and a review cadence.
```
