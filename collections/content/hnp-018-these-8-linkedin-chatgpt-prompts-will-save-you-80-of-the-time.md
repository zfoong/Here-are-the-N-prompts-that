# ✍️ These 8 LinkedIn ChatGPT prompts will save you 80% of the time

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ai-future-skills_these-8-linkedin-chatgpt-prompts-will-save-activity-7442676701890850817-gJxv)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Post idea generator

```text
Generate ten LinkedIn ideas for [NICHE] and [AUDIENCE], each tied to a real problem, distinct angle, suitable format, and proof I should provide.
```

### 2. High-engagement draft

```text
Draft a LinkedIn post about [TOPIC] from [SOURCE_NOTES] with a specific hook, clear point, concrete example, and non-manipulative CTA.
```

### 3. Storytelling conversion

```text
Turn [IDEA] into a story-led outline using context, tension, decision, result, and lesson. Do not invent scenes or outcomes.
```

### 4. Engagement edit

```text
Edit [POST] for clarity, concision, specificity, and natural rhythm. Preserve voice and facts; show removed filler.
```

### 5. Carousel outline

```text
Create a [SLIDE_COUNT]-slide LinkedIn carousel outline on [TOPIC] with one idea per slide, visual cue, source note, and final takeaway.
```

### 6. Hook options

```text
Write five credible hooks for [TOPIC] using different mechanisms. Keep each specific and exclude hype unsupported by [EVIDENCE].
```

### 7. Content repurposing

```text
Adapt [SOURCE_CONTENT] into a LinkedIn post for [AUDIENCE]. Preserve attribution and core meaning while making structure native to the platform.
```

### 8. Clarity optimizer

```text
Review [POST] for unclear claims, weak transitions, jargon, and missing evidence; produce an improved draft plus a short change log.
```
