# 10 prompts to plan, write, and grow your Facebook page

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/altiam.artificialintelligence/posts/use-these-10-prompts-to-plan-write-and-grow-your-facebook-page-faster-/122169864674620880/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. 14-day content plan

```text
Create a 14 day Facebook content plan for [audience] to get [outcome]. 1 post per day. Include hook + key points + CTA.
```

### 2. 20 post ideas per pillar

```text
Give me 20 post ideas for pillar [pillar]. Each idea must include an example and a clear takeaway.
```

### 3. 25 short hooks

```text
Write 25 short hooks for [topic]. Style: simple, direct. No fluff. Focus on the result.
```

### 4. Rough note to post

```text
Turn this rough note into a Facebook post: [paste]. Keep it human, short lines, and add 1 mini example.
```

### 5. Ethical comment prompts

```text
Suggest 10 ethical comment prompts for this post to start real discussion: [paste post].
```

### 6. 35-second Reel script

```text
Write a 35 sec Reel script on [topic]. Hook in 1 sentence, 3 steps, 1 example, 1 CTA.
```

### 7. 7-slide carousel outline

```text
Convert this into a 7 slide carousel outline: [paste]. Each slide has 1 idea and 1 line max.
```

### 8. Repurpose one post

```text
Repurpose this post into: 1 Reel script, 1 carousel, 3 story prompts: [paste].
```

### 9. 15 reply templates

```text
Draft 15 reply templates for comments like: praise, question, disagreement, asking for link. Tone: friendly.
```

### 10. Metrics to experiments

```text
Based on these metrics [reach, saves, shares, comments], propose 5 experiments for next week and rewrite 3 posts.
```
