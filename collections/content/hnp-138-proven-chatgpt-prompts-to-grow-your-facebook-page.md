# Proven ChatGPT prompts to grow your Facebook page

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/simplydigital.gr/posts/proven-chatgpt-prompts-to-grow-your-facebook-page-ready-to-paste-and-use/1393205789486543/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Viral Hook Generator

```text
You are a viral Facebook copywriter. Give me 12 scroll-stopping hooks (5–12 words) for a post about [insert topic], each one curiosity-driven and punchy. Include one emoji per hook and make them feel native to Facebook.
```

### 2. Save-Worthy Carousel Builder

```text
Act as a Facebook content strategist. Create a 7-slide carousel for [insert topic]. Give me: a headline for each slide, 1–2 bite-sized points of value, and a suggested visual idea. End with a save-worthy CTA.
```

### 3. Short Educational Post (High Reach)

```text
Write a Facebook post of 120–200 words teaching [insert practical skill]. Start with a strong hook, give 3 clear steps, one quick example, and finish with a question that invites comments.
```

### 4. Story Post That Builds Connection

```text
Write a relatable Facebook story (200–300 words) about a small mistake related to [insert topic]. Add the lesson you learned and end with a question to spark replies.
```

### 5. Advanced Viral Hook Enhancer

```text
Act as a hook optimization expert. Take this hook: [paste hook] and give me 10 stronger versions using these angles: curiosity, tension, polarizing statement, open loop, "mistake to avoid," unexpected fact, and emotional appeal. Keep each under 12 words.
```

### 6. Caption Variations for A/B Testing

```text
Take this caption idea: [paste caption]. Rewrite it into 5 variations: curiosity-driven, emotional, short & punchy, value-focused, and community-building. Keep each under 140 characters.
```

### 7. Comment Reply Pack (Boost Engagement)

```text
Generate 12 short, human replies to the comment: [paste comment]. Include: 2 witty, 4 helpful, 4 clarifying questions, and 2 uplifting replies. Keep them under 30 words each
```

### 8. Facebook Poll Ideas That Spark Conversation

```text
Give me 8 poll ideas for an audience interested in [insert niche]. Each poll should have two options and one follow-up question to post in the comments to drive more replies.
```

### 9. One-Month Content Calendar

```text
Create a 4-week Facebook content plan for [insert topic] with 3 posts per week. For each post, include: format (carousel, text, video), a headline, the angle of the post, and a short CTA.
```

### 10. Repurposing Plan (Turn 1 Video Into 8 Posts)

```text
I have a 10-minute video about [insert topic]. Turn it into 8 Facebook pieces: 4 short clip ideas, 2 carousel ideas, 1 text post idea, and 1 engagement-focused question.
```
