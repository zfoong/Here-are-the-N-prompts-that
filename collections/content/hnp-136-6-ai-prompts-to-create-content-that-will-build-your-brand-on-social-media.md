# 6 AI prompts to create content that will build your brand on social media

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/6-ai-prompts-to-create-content-that-will-build-your-brand-on-social-media/25912014858404670/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The "Myth Buster" Post

```text
Act as a Social Media Strategist and Subject Matter Expert in [Insert Your Industry].

I want to write a social media post for [Insert Platform, e.g., LinkedIn/Instagram] targeting [Insert Target Audience]. There is a common misconception in our industry that [Insert Common Myth, e.g., "you need a lot of money to start investing"].

Please write a post that debunks this myth. Explain why it is incorrect and provide the truth instead. The tone should be helpful, educational, and authoritative. Do not include a call to action to buy anything; simply ask a question to encourage discussion in the comments.

Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. The "Actionable Quick Win" Guide

```text
Act as an Educational Content Creator for the [Insert Your Industry] sector.

My audience consists of [Insert Target Audience] who often struggle with [Insert Specific Pain Point]. I want to give them a "quick win"—something they can do in under 15 minutes to improve their situation.

Write a social media post that outlines a 3-step process to solve this specific problem. The steps should be clear, concise, and immediately actionable. The goal is pure utility and helpfulness. Ensure the tone is encouraging and strictly non-promotional.

Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. The "Industry Trend" Analysis

```text
Act as an Industry Analyst and Thought Leader in [Insert Your Industry].

There is currently a trend or news event regarding [Insert Trend/News Topic]. I want to provide a thoughtful perspective on this for my network on [Insert Platform].

Write a post that summarizes this trend and offers a unique insight or prediction about how this will impact [Insert Target Audience] over the next 6 months. Focus on strategic advice and high-level thinking. Avoid any sales language; the goal is to be seen as a trusted advisor.

Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. The "Mistake & Lesson" Story (Vulnerability)

```text
Act as a Brand Storyteller and Expert Coach.

I want to share a story about a mistake I made early in my career regarding [Insert Topic/Skill]. The mistake was [Insert Brief Description of Mistake].

Write a narrative social media post that describes this mistake, the consequences, and most importantly, the valuable lesson I learned from it. Frame this as advice to [Insert Target Audience] so they can avoid making the same error. The tone should be humble yet wise. Do not sell any services; just share the lesson.

Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. The "Curated Resource" List

```text
Act as a Helpful Curator and Mentor in the [Insert Your Industry] space.

I want to create a "Save for Later" style post for [Insert Target Audience]. Please compile a list of 5 essential tools, books, or resources that help with [Insert Specific Task or Goal].

For each item, write one sentence explaining why it is valuable. The post should be formatted for easy reading (using emojis or bullet points). The goal is to be the most helpful person in their feed today. No self-promotion, just genuine resource sharing.

Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. The "Behind the Scenes" Process

```text
Act as a Documentary Narrator and Process Expert.

I want to show [Insert Target Audience] how we handle [Insert Specific Process/Task, e.g., "onboarding a new client" or "sourcing materials"].

Write a social media post that takes the reader behind the scenes of this process. Focus on the "why" behind the steps we take—specifically how [Insert Specific Detail] ensures a better result or safety. The goal is to be transparent and educational about how things work in our industry. Keep it fascinating and educational, not salesy.

Ask me any questions you have.
```
