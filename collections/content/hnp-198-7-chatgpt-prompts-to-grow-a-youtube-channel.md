# 7 ChatGPT Prompts to Grow a YouTube Channel

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/LoganMillerVideos/posts/chatgpt-youtube-100k-views-in-30-daysuse-these-7-prompts-to-script-optimize-and-/972725345721283/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Video Script Generator

```text
Write a detailed script for a [niche/topic] YouTube video targeting [specific audience]. Include a strong hook, key talking points, and a clear call to action.
```

### 2. Trending Topic Finder

```text
Give me 10 trending video ideas in the [niche] category with high viewer demand and low competition for [current month/year].
```

### 3. SEO Title & Description

```text
Create an SEO-optimized YouTube title and description for a video about [topic], with keywords to boost search visibility.
```

### 4. Thumbnail Text Ideas

```text
Suggest 5 attention-grabbing thumbnail text options for a YouTube video on [topic] to boost click-through rates.
```

### 5. Audience Engagement Script

```text
Write a few engagement prompts, questions and calls-to-action, I can use in my video to drive comments, likes, and subscriptions from [target audience].
```

### 6. Video Upload Schedule

```text
Build a consistent upload schedule for a YouTube channel in [niche] that's aiming for 100K views within 30 days.
```

### 7. Analytics Review Guide

```text
Explain how to read YouTube analytics for a [niche] channel to spot top-performing content and optimize future videos for growth.
```
