# From zero to viral: 7 ChatGPT prompts to explode your Threads growth in 30 days

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/from-zero-to-viral-7-chatgpt-prompts-to-explode-your-threads-growth-in-30-daysno/10173946081745347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Viral Hook Creation

```text
Give me 20 viral-style hooks tailored for Threads posts about [your niche], written in a short, conversational style that encourages replies.
```

### 2. Audience Magnet Topics

```text
List trending, under-discussed topics in [your niche] that are likely to spark debate, curiosity, or conversation on Threads.
```

### 3. Content Remix Formula

```text
Take this old tweet/post [paste text] and rewrite it into 5 fresh, engaging Threads versions optimized for interaction.
```

### 4. Conversational Thread Starters

```text
Generate 10 short, curiosity-driven questions I can post on Threads to invite engagement from my target audience in [niche].
```

### 5. Storytelling That Scales

```text
Turn this personal story [insert details] into a high-impact Thread post that teaches a lesson while staying relatable and authentic.
```

### 6. Authority Builder

```text
Create 7 bite-sized, actionable tips in [your niche] that position me as an expert while being easy for readers to share or respond to.
```

### 7. Viral Engagement Engine

```text
Suggest 10 ways to reply to trending Threads posts in [your niche] that add value, showcase expertise, and attract profile clicks.
```
