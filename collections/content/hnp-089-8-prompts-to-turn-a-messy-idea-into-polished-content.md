# Turn one messy idea into polished content in 60 minutes

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPTPromptGenius/comments/1n7z7x4)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Brief Builder

```text
Act as a creative producer. Ask me 7 questions to clarify audience, goal, angle, and proof for a post about [TOPIC]. Then summarise a one paragraph brief plus a 5 point checklist.
```

### 2. Angle Finder

```text
You're a messaging strategist for [NICHE]. Generate 12 content angles grouped by educate, counterintuitive, step by step, story, and quick win. One line per angle, no fluff.
```

### 3. Outline With Word Counts

```text
Act as an editor. Create a structured outline for a post on [TOPIC] with section headings and target word counts totalling 250 to 400 words. Add a suggested title.
```

### 4. First Draft, Mobile Ready

```text
You're a social copywriter. Write the post from the outline for [PLATFORM]. Short lines, clear transitions, Australian English, one core idea only.
```

### 5. Voice Match Pass

```text
Act as a ghostwriter. Analyse this sample of my writing, then rewrite the draft to match my tone and rhythm. Keep the structure the same. [PASTE SAMPLE]
```

### 6. Proof Injection

```text
You're a research assistant. Add two specific examples, stats, or mini case notes that support the main claim for [AUDIENCE]. Cite the source in plain text, no links.
```

### 7. Tighten and Trim

```text
Act as a clarity editor. Cut filler, remove repeated ideas, cap sentences at 20 words, keep total under 280 words. Return only the revised post.
```

### 8. CTA Fit Test

```text
You're a conversion writer. Provide 5 CTA options that feel natural for this post, each targeting one goal: saves, shares, clicks, DMs, or bookings.
```
