# 5 writing prompts to unlock ChatGPT's full potential

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/5-writing-prompts-to-unlock-chatgpts-full-potentialsave-these-theyll-completely-/10173745997665347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The Voice Customizer Prompt

```text
Rewrite this paragraph in a tone that's [conversational / witty / professional / emotional], like I'm talking to [a specific person or audience]. Keep it natural.
```

### 2. The Content Expansion Prompt

```text
Take this short idea and expand it into a full-length post with examples, a personal story, and a takeaway: [insert idea].
```

### 3. The Audience Hook Prompt

```text
Give me 5 attention-grabbing opening lines for a [blog post / email / Instagram caption] about [topic], written to hook [ideal audience].
```

### 4. The Rewrite Without Losing My Voice Prompt

```text
Improve this writing for flow, grammar, and clarity — but keep my tone and make it sound like me. [Paste your text]
```

### 5. The Format Converter Prompt

```text
Turn this blog post into an Instagram carousel / LinkedIn post / email newsletter — highlight key points, break it into sections, and make it skimmable.
```
