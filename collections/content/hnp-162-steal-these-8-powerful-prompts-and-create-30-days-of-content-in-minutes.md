# Steal these 8 powerful prompts and create 30 days of content in minutes

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/simplydigital.gr/posts/steal-these-8-powerful-prompts-and-create-a-30-days-content-in-minutes/1329619065845216/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Prompt 1

```text
Act as my content strategist. Create a 30-day content plan for TikTok + Instagram based on my niche: [insert niche]. Include: hooks, angles, emotional triggers, and formats (reels, carousels, quotes). Keep everything short, high-retention, and scroll-stopping.
```

### 2. Prompt 2

```text
Act as my scriptwriter. Turn this topic into a viral 20–35 sec reel script with a strong hook, a punchy middle, and a confident CTA at the end: [insert topic].
```

### 3. Prompt 3

```text
Act as a high-end editor. Rewrite this script to be sharper, quicker, and more 'thumb-stopping.' Remove fluff. Add tension and pattern interrupts: [paste script].
```

### 4. Prompt 4

```text
Act as a design consultant. Give me a carousel outline for Instagram for this topic: [insert topic]. Include slide-by-slide text, layout suggestions, and a bold headline.
```

### 5. Prompt 5

```text
Act as a market researcher. Give me 20 viral content ideas for my niche: [insert niche] based on current trends, audience frustrations, and income-driven motivations.
```

### 6. Prompt 6

```text
Act as my brand voice creator. Rewrite this caption in a bold, confident, no-nonsense tone that matches top creators: [paste caption].
```

### 7. Prompt 7

```text
Act as a content repurposing engine. Turn this long-form content into 5 reels, 5 carousels, and 5 quote posts: [paste YouTube video, blog, or text].
```

### 8. Prompt 8

```text
Act as a social media optimizer. Analyze these 3 posts and tell me how to improve the hook, retention, storytelling, and CTA: [paste 3 posts].
```
