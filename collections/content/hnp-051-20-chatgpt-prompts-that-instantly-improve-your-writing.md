# 20 ChatGPT Prompts that Instantly Improve your Writing

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/promptingmagic/comments/1r6qa27/20_chatgpt_prompts_that_instantly_improve_your)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Clarity and Precision Writing

```text
You are an expert editor and communicator. Rewrite the following text to maximize clarity, precision, and readability while preserving the original intent and meaning. Eliminate ambiguity, tighten sentences, simplify complex phrasing, and ensure that each sentence communicates a single clear idea. Target an intelligent but non-expert reader. Text to rewrite: [paste text]
```

### 2. Audience-First Rewriting

```text
Rewrite the following content specifically for the audience described below. Adjust vocabulary, examples, tone, and depth to match their knowledge level, motivations, and pain points. Ensure the writing feels relevant, engaging, and personalized for them. Original text: [paste text] Audience description: [define audience clearly]
```

### 3. Voice Consistency

```text
Rewrite the following content so that it maintains a consistent voice and style throughout. Ensure the voice matches this description: [describe voice, e.g., confident, calm, analytical, witty]. Text to revise: [paste text]
```

### 4. Clarity Through Examples

```text
Rewrite the following text by adding clear, concrete examples where they would improve understanding. Ensure examples are relevant and easy to grasp. Original text: [paste text]
```

### 5. Persuasive Writing Enhancement

```text
Act as a persuasion expert. Rewrite the following text to make it more convincing by strengthening the core argument, clarifying the value proposition, and aligning the message with the reader's incentives and objections. Use logic, subtle emotional appeal, and credibility signals. Text to enhance: [paste text] Desired outcome from reader: [action or belief]
```

### 6. Tone Transformation

```text
Rewrite the following text while keeping the meaning intact but changing the tone to the one specified below. Ensure the tone is consistent throughout and appropriate for the context. Original text: [paste text] New tone: [e.g., professional, friendly, authoritative, inspiring]
```

### 7. Idea Expansion

```text
Act as a thought partner and writer. Expand the core idea below into a complete, well-articulated piece by adding explanations, examples, analogies, and logical connections where helpful. Do not add unrelated ideas. Core idea or rough notes: [paste idea]
```

### 8. Storytelling Enhancement

```text
Rewrite the following content using storytelling techniques such as context setting, cause and effect, tension, and resolution while keeping it factual and relevant. Do not exaggerate or fictionalize. Original text: [paste text]
```

### 9. Final Quality Control

```text
Act as a strict editor performing a final quality check. Review the following text and rewrite it to correct any remaining issues related to clarity, structure, tone, or flow. The goal is to produce a publication-ready version. Text to review: [paste text]
```

### 10. Redundancy and Repetition Cleanup

```text
Rewrite the following text to eliminate repetitive ideas, phrases, or examples. Ensure each paragraph adds new value and moves the piece forward. Original text: [paste text]
```

### 11. Plain English Simplification

```text
Rewrite the following content in plain, simple English while keeping it accurate. Avoid jargon where possible, explain necessary technical terms briefly, and prioritize ease of understanding. Original text: [paste text] Target reading level: [e.g., general audience, Grade 8]
```

### 12. Logical Flow Improvement

```text
Review the following text and rewrite it to improve logical flow and coherence. Reorder sentences or paragraphs if necessary so ideas progress naturally from one to the next. Ensure there are no logical gaps or abrupt transitions. Text to improve: [paste text]
```

### 13. Professional Polishing

```text
Act as a senior editor polishing a final draft. Improve sentence structure, word choice, grammar, and overall professionalism of the following text without changing its core message. Text to polish: [paste text]
```

### 14. Argument Strengthening

```text
Analyze the argument presented in the following text and rewrite it to make the reasoning tighter and more defensible. Clarify assumptions, strengthen evidence, and remove weak or unsupported claims. Text to revise: [paste text]
```

### 15. Platform Specific Writing

```text
Rewrite the following content specifically for the platform mentioned below. Adjust structure, length, tone, and formatting to match what performs best on that platform. Text to adapt: [paste text] Platform: [e.g., LinkedIn, Twitter thread, Blog post, Newsletter]
```

### 16. Strong Conclusion

```text
Rewrite the conclusion of the following text to leave the reader with a clear takeaway, reinforced main idea, or call to action. The conclusion should feel satisfying and purposeful rather than abrupt. Conclusion text: [paste conclusion]
```

### 17. Headline and Subheading Writing

```text
Create a compelling headline and supporting subheadings for the following content. The headline should be clear and attention-grabbing, while subheadings should guide the reader logically through the piece. Content summary or draft: [paste text]
```

### 18. Conciseness Optimization

```text
You are a senior editor known for concise writing. Rewrite the following text to reduce length without losing meaning, nuance, or important details. Remove redundancy, filler phrases, and unnecessary explanations while maintaining a natural flow. Original text: [paste text] Target length or reduction percentage: [optional]
```

### 19. Structured Long Form Writing

```text
Act as a professional writer and strategist. Using the topic below, create a well-structured long-form piece with a clear introduction, logically ordered sections, smooth transitions, and a strong conclusion. Each section should build on the previous one and contribute to a single cohesive argument or narrative. Topic: [insert topic] Target audience: [describe audience] Desired tone: [formal, conversational, persuasive]
```

### 20. The "Devil's Advocate" Critique

```text
Review the text below and act as a critical opponent. Identify weaknesses, unanswered questions, or logical fallacies in the writing. Then, rewrite the sections that are weak to address these counter-arguments preemptively. Text to critique: [paste text]
```
