# ✍️ ChatGPT has 180,000,000+ users. Here are 7 prompts to unlock creativity.

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bensonsung_chatgpt-has-180000000-users-but-almost-activity-7137840564376387584-U8nN)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Improve a hook

```text
Review this thread hook [HOOK] for clarity, specificity, tension, and credibility. Offer ten alternatives and explain the trade-off in the top three.
```

### 2. Generate thread ideas

```text
Generate [COUNT] thread ideas for [NICHE] about [TOPIC], each with a distinct audience problem, angle, and evidence I would need.
```

### 3. LinkedIn post hook

```text
Write 12 [TONE] LinkedIn hooks for [TOPIC], each under [WORD_LIMIT] words and grounded in [SOURCE_MATERIAL].
```

### 4. Analogy post

```text
Create three concise analogies for [TOPIC] using [FAMILIAR_DOMAIN], and state where each analogy stops being accurate.
```

### 5. Authenticity-building post

```text
Draft a short post about [REAL_EXPERIENCE] in [TOPIC] that separates what happened, what I learned, and what remains uncertain.
```

### 6. Aspirational thread

```text
Outline an aspirational thread about lessons from [EXPERIENCE]. Keep the claims proportional to the evidence and include concrete takeaways.
```

### 7. Actionable quote post

```text
Turn [QUOTE_OR_IDEA] into a short response that adds one practical action, one example, and respectful attribution.
```
