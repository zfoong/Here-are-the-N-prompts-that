# Stop telling ChatGPT to write like a human: 7 smarter prompts

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/aitrendz.xyz1/posts/stop-telling-chatgpt-to-write-like-a-humantry-these-7-smarter-prompts-that-actua/122170382582603416/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Tone Calibration Tool

```text
Here's my draft: [insert text]. Rewrite it to sound confident, natural, and conversational, like a real person explaining it to a friend.
```

### 2. Clarity Overhaul

```text
Here's my content: [insert text]. Rewrite it for maximum clarity. Use shorter sentences, remove fluff, and make every word count.
```

### 3. Reader Reaction Check

```text
Read this as if you're my ideal reader. What parts confuse, bore, or lose trust, and how would you fix them?
```

### 4. Structure Fixer

```text
Reorganize this content so it flows logically and keeps attention. Add clear transitions, headlines, and natural pacing.
```

### 5. Specificity Upgrade

```text
Make this content more specific and concrete. Replace vague phrases with examples, details, or numbers where possible.
```

### 6. Natural Dialogue Rewriter

```text
Turn this block of text into a more casual, back-and-forth conversation between two people, keep it authentic and human.
```

### 7. Emotional Hook Enhancer

```text
Strengthen the emotional pull of this message. Make the reader feel understood, curious, or inspired within the first 3 lines.
```
