# ✍️ Here are 7 ChatGPT prompts to turn AI into your personal assistant

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/reneremsik_everyones-worried-ai-will-take-their-jobs-activity-7391493022221500416-kNik)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Content strategist

```text
For my niche [NICHE], audience [AUDIENCE], voice [VOICE_SAMPLE], and goal [GOAL], generate ten content ideas with angle, format, and proof needed.
```

### 2. Delegation audit

```text
Classify my weekly tasks [TASKS] as keep, automate, delegate, batch, or stop. Explain the decision, risks, setup cost, and next action.
```

### 3. Conversational editor

```text
Edit [TEXT] to sound conversational but professional. Preserve my claims and voice, shorten awkward phrasing, and show substantive edits.
```

### 4. One-week content calendar

```text
Create a one-week calendar for [PLATFORM] aligned with [GOAL], including hooks, outline, CTA, asset needed, and success signal for each post.
```

### 5. Beginner explainer

```text
Explain [CONCEPT] to a beginner using a simple model and three examples, then list the important nuance the simplification hides.
```

### 6. Results-driven resume bullets

```text
Rewrite [BULLETS] to foreground action and measured result. Ask for missing evidence and never invent scope, numbers, or tools.
```

### 7. Accountability routine

```text
Build a realistic daily routine for [GOAL] around [CONSTRAINTS], with minimum action, trigger, tracking method, recovery rule, and weekly review.
```
