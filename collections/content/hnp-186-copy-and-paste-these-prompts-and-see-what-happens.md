# Copy and paste these prompts and see what happens

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/simplydigital.gr/posts/copy-and-paste-these-prompts-and-see-what-happens/1437270525080069/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Viral Hook Generator

```text
Give me 30 scroll-stopping hooks for [my niche]. Make them curiosity-driven, short, and optimized for social media. Avoid clichés and make each one feel unexpected.
```

### 2. Content Ideas Machine

```text
Generate 30 content ideas for [my niche] that are highly engaging and practical. Mix educational, controversial, and storytelling formats. Prioritize ideas that can go viral.
```

### 3. Audience Pain Points Extractor

```text
List the top 20 problems, frustrations, and desires of people interested in [my niche]. Turn each into a content idea with a strong angle.
```

### 4. Carousel/Post Builder

```text
Turn this idea into a viral carousel post: [insert idea]. Include a strong hook, 5–7 slides, and a powerful ending CTA. Keep sentences short and impactful.
```

### 5. Short Video Script Generator

```text
Write a 30–60 second viral video script about [topic]. Include a hook in the first 3 seconds, clear value, and a punchy ending. Make it sound natural and engaging.
```

### 6. Contrarian Content Creator

```text
Give me 10 controversial or contrarian takes in [my niche] that challenge common beliefs. Make them bold but logical and defensible.
```

### 7. Trend Hijacker

```text
Based on current trends in [my niche], suggest 15 content ideas I can create right now. Focus on topics gaining attention or momentum.
```

### 8. Content Repurposing Engine

```text
Take this piece of content: [insert content] Turn it into 5 different formats (tweet, reel script, carousel, hook list, caption). Optimize each for engagement.
```
