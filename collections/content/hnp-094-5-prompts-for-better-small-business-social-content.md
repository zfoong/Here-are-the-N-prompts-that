# 5 Prompts for Better Small-Business Social Content

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ZenBusinessInc/comments/1o0rxf5)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Behind-the-Scenes

```text
Share a photo or short story about what a day in your business looks like.
```

### 2. Customer Value

```text
What’s one tip or trick your customers could use today that relates to your product or service?
```

### 3. Origin Story

```text
Explain why you started your business in 3 sentences.
```

### 4. Problem/Solution

```text
Think of the #1 pain point your customer has. Write a post showing how you help solve it.
```

### 5. Engagement

```text
Ask your followers a simple either/or question related to your niche (e.g., coffee vs. tea, DIY vs. hire a pro).
```
