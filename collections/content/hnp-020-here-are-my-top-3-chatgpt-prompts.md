## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. STAR repurposer

```text
Rewrite [SOURCE_CONTENT] as a concise LinkedIn story using situation, task, action, and result. Use simple language, preserve facts, avoid emojis, and ask up to five clarifying questions first.
```

### 2. Prompt improver

```text
Before answering [DRAFT_PROMPT], rewrite it to clarify the goal, context, inputs, constraints, and output format; then explain the three most consequential changes.
```

### 3. Polite closer

```text
End the interaction with a brief, courteous acknowledgement. This changes tone, not model capability; do not treat politeness as a reliability technique.
```
