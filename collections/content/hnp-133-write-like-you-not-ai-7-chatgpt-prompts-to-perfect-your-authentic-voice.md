# Write like you, not AI: 7 ChatGPT prompts to perfect your authentic voice

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/write-like-you-not-ai-7-chatgpt-prompts-to-perfect-your-authentic-voice-stop-sou/10174294370550347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Voice Mirror Prompt

```text
ChatGPT, analyze this sample of my writing [paste text] and break down the key elements that make it sound like me (tone, rhythm, word choice, pacing). Then, explain how I can apply these traits to all my future content.
```

### 2. Tone Translator Prompt

```text
ChatGPT, take this draft I wrote [paste text] and rewrite it in my authentic voice. Keep the message the same, but make it sound more natural, conversational, and aligned with how I actually talk.
```

### 3. Authenticity Filter Prompt

```text
ChatGPT, review this piece of content [paste text] and highlight any sentences that sound too generic, cliché, or robotic. Suggest alternative phrasings that feel more personal and true to my voice.
```

### 4. Personal Story Infusion Prompt

```text
ChatGPT, suggest 3 ways I could naturally weave in personal anecdotes or lived experiences to this piece of content [paste text]. Make sure they support the main idea and feel authentic—not forced.
```

### 5. Word Swap Prompt

```text
ChatGPT, identify 10 words or phrases I overuse in my writing and give me fresh alternatives that still sound like me. Also suggest 5 signature words/phrases I could consistently use to make my voice stand out.
```

### 6. Audience Resonance Prompt

```text
ChatGPT, rewrite this draft [paste text] in my authentic voice but tuned to resonate more with my audience [describe your audience]. Suggest ways to balance my natural tone with what feels relatable to them.
```

### 7. Voice Evolution Prompt

```text
ChatGPT, based on my current writing style [paste sample], show me how I can refine it to feel even more authentic while also being persuasive, engaging, and memorable. Give me a roadmap for leveling up my voice over the next 3 months.
```
