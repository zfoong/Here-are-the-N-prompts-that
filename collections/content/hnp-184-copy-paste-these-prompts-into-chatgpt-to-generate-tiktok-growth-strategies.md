# Copy & paste these prompts into ChatGPT to generate TikTok growth strategies

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/copy-paste-these-prompts-into-chatgptlet-it-generate-tiktok-growth-strategies-yo/10173835081270347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Create a 7-day TikTok content plan

```text
Act as a TikTok strategist. Create a 7-day content plan for a [insert niche] account. Include hooks, trends to use, and CTA ideas for each day.
```

### 2. Find viral trends in my niche

```text
Give me a list of recent viral TikTok trends I can use for a [insert niche] brand. Include how I can put my own spin on them.
```

### 3. Turn long content into short-form gold

```text
Repurpose this blog/video/podcast [paste content] into 3 TikTok ideas with a scroll-stopping hook and engaging delivery.
```

### 4. Boost engagement with comment-worthy content

```text
Write 5 TikTok video ideas that are guaranteed to spark conversation, reactions, or shares for an audience interested in [topic].
```

### 5. Optimize my profile for growth

```text
Review and rewrite my TikTok bio, name, and link strategy to make it more searchable, memorable, and growth-oriented in the [niche] space.
```
