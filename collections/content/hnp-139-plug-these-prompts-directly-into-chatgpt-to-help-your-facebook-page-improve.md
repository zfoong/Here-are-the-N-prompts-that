# Plug these prompts directly into ChatGPT to help your Facebook page improve

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/simplydigital.gr/posts/plug-these-prompts-directly-into-chatgpt-to-help-your-facebook-page-improve/1385689990238123/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The Scroll-Stopping Hook Generator

```text
I want 15 scroll-stopping hooks for a Facebook post. The topic is: [insert topic]. Write them in a punchy, curiosity-driven style that forces a pause in the feed. Keep each hook 1–2 lines max. Avoid hype words like 'insane' or 'guaranteed.' Make them feel like something real creators would use, not generic marketing lines. Prioritise tension, contrast, bold statements, and pattern interrupts
```

### 2. The Viral Facebook Idea Generator (AI-related)

```text
Give me 20 viral Facebook post ideas about AI. They should be: practical, surprising, based on real-world use cases, and easy for everyday people to relate to. Prioritise ideas that spark strong reactions: curiosity, disagreement, 'I need to try this,' or 'nobody told me this.' Each idea should include: a title, a one-sentence angle, why it would go viral (1 line). Make sure they feel native to Facebook, not LinkedIn.
```

### 3. The High-Converting Lead Gen Angle Generator

```text
I need 12 Facebook post angles that naturally generate leads for [insert your service/product] without sounding salesy. Build angles around: transformations, mistakes to avoid, behind-the-scenes, step-by-step processes, 'I tried this so you don't have to', mini case studies. Each angle should include a soft CTA that invites people to comment or DM for more details.
```

### 4. The "Facebook Algorithm Expert" Optimizer

```text
Act as a Facebook algorithm strategist. I'll paste a post. You'll analyse it and: 1. Rewrite it for maximum reach 2. Improve the hook 3. Add story elements where needed 4. Adjust structure for retention 5. Suggest the ideal CTA 6. Tell me EXACTLY what makes this optimized version work based on 2025 engagement signals: watch time, comment depth, share intention, dwell time, re-engagement signals. Give me the optimized post + a breakdown.
```
