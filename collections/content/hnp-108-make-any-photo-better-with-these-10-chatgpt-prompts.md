# Make any photo better with these 10 ChatGPT prompts

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/SimplyDigital.gr/posts/make-any-photo-better-with-these-10-chatgpt-prompts/1496285382511916/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Remove Background

```text
Remove the background from this image and make it transparent. Preserve all details of the main subject and create clean, professional edges.
```

### 2. Enhance Image Quality

```text
Enhance this image by increasing sharpness, improving lighting, reducing noise, and boosting overall quality while keeping it natural and realistic.
```

### 3. Change Background

```text
Replace the background with [describe background]. Keep the subject exactly the same and ensure realistic lighting, shadows, and depth.
```

### 4. Color Correction

```text
Adjust the colors, contrast, brightness, and white balance of this image to make it look vibrant, professional, and visually appealing.
```

### 5. Remove Objects

```text
Remove the [object/person] from this image and naturally fill the empty space so there are no visible editing traces.
```

### 6. Add Text to Image

```text
Add the text '[YOUR TEXT]' to this image using a bold, modern font. Position it professionally and ensure it complements the overall design.
```

### 7. Change Colors

```text
Change the color of [object] to [new color] while maintaining realistic lighting, reflections, textures, and shadows.
```

### 8. Resize Image

```text
Resize this image to [dimensions/aspect ratio] while maintaining maximum quality, sharpness, and proper composition.
```

### 9. Add Realistic Shadow

```text
Add realistic shadows to the subject so it blends naturally into the scene with accurate lighting direction and depth.
```

### 10. Transform Image Style

```text
Transform this image into a [cinematic/anime/3D render/oil painting/illustration] style while preserving the subject and key details.
```
