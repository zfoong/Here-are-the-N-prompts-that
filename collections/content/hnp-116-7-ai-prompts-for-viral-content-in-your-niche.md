# 7 AI prompts for viral content in your niche

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Content & Marketing** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/7-ai-prompts-for-viral-content-in-your-niche-/24882983274641172/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. "X to Y" Transformation Framework

```text
Act as a viral content strategist. You specialize in creating hooks that grab attention on social media. I'm in the [niche] space and want to showcase relatable before/after moments that highlight wins or changes. Give me 10 "X → Y" transformation one-liners that I can use as hooks in posts and videos. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. Swipe File of Proven CTAs

```text
Act as a direct-response copywriter. You know how to craft CTAs that drive clicks, signups, and conversions. I create content in [niche] and need strong calls-to-action that balance engagement and sales. List 15 proven CTA formulas I can adapt for my posts. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. Controversy with Class

```text
Act as a thought leadership coach. You help creators stand out with unique perspectives. I want to share contrarian takes in [niche] that challenge common beliefs without being offensive. Write 5 contrarian post ideas that spark curiosity and discussion. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. Content Remix Machine

```text
Act as a social media repurposing expert. You know how to turn one idea into multiple formats across platforms. Take one of my top-performing posts in [niche] and give me 7 remix angles (thread, listicle, story, infographic, short-form, etc.) tailored to Twitter, TikTok, and LinkedIn. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. Mini-Case Studies

```text
Act as a content marketer. You specialize in showing authority through storytelling. I'm in [niche] and want to share social proof in a concise way. Write 3 short case study-style posts (under 150 words) that show problem → solution → result. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 6. Engagement Traps That Work

```text
Act as a community engagement strategist. You know how to spark conversations that feel authentic. I want to build audience interaction in [niche]. Give me 10 natural, curiosity-driven questions I can post to increase comments and discussions. Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 7. Offer Positioning Made Easy

```text
Act as a conversion copywriter. You specialize in positioning offers as the natural next step. I want to promote my [offer/product/service] in [niche] without sounding pushy. Write 5 posts that naturally position my offer as the obvious follow-up after consuming my free content. Ask me any questions you have.
```
