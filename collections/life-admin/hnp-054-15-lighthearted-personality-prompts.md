# 15 Goldilocks prompts: not too deep, not too silly, just right

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Life Admin** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPT/comments/1rcywen/15_goldilocks_prompts_not_too_deep_not_too_silly)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Prompt 1

```text
What is a completely unnecessary object I own that somehow represents me perfectly?
```

### 2. Prompt 2

```text
If my personality were a room, what would be in it—and what would be strangely missing?
```

### 3. Prompt 3

```text
What is something I do slower than most people, and why might that actually be a strength?
```

### 4. Prompt 4

```text
What “phase” of my life never fully ended—it just went quiet for a while?
```

### 5. Prompt 5

```text
What kind of boredom is most dangerous for me: restless boredom or comfortable boredom?
```

### 6. Prompt 6

```text
If my brain were optimized like a workspace, what three things would be within arm’s reach at all times?
```

### 7. Prompt 7

```text
What am I unusually good at noticing that other people tend to miss?
```

### 8. Prompt 8

```text
What’s something small that reliably resets my mood when I’m off?
```

### 9. Prompt 9

```text
If I were famous for something unrelated to my career, what would it most likely be?
```

### 10. Prompt 10

```text
What kind of mess stresses me out the most: physical mess, time mess, decision mess, or emotional mess?
```

### 11. Prompt 11

```text
What do I outgrow faster than most people?
```

### 12. Prompt 12

```text
What is something I’ve quietly become much better at over the past five years?
```

### 13. Prompt 13

```text
What environment brings out the most “me” version of me?
```

### 14. Prompt 14

```text
What’s something I take seriously that I would probably enjoy more if I treated it like a game?
```

### 15. Prompt 15

```text
What future version of me would be most surprised by how I spend my free time?
```
