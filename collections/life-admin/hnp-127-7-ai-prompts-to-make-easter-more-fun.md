# 7 AI prompts to make Easter more fun

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Life Admin** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/Owwstin/posts/happy-easter-here-are-7-ai-prompts-to-make-today-more-fun1-easter-egg-hunt-desig/10161951480626256/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Easter Egg Hunt Designer

```text
Design a creative Easter egg hunt for [number] kids aged [ages] in a [backyard/house/park]. Give me 10 unique hiding spots, 5 riddle clues that lead to a golden egg, and a point system to make it competitive.
```

### 2. AI Easter Basket Builder

```text
I need Easter basket ideas for [person/age]. Budget is $[X]. Search for trending gifts right now that aren't just candy. Give me a full basket with links to buy everything.
```

### 3. Family Photo Prompter

```text
Give me 15 creative family photo pose ideas for Easter Sunday. Include some funny ones, some wholesome ones, and some that work for large groups. Factor in that we'll have [describe setting].
```

### 4. Easter Brunch Menu

```text
Plan an Easter brunch for [X] people with a budget of $[X]. Give me a full menu with appetizers, mains, desserts, and a signature cocktail. Include a prep timeline so everything is ready by noon.
```

### 5. Peeps Debate Settler

```text
Give me a detailed, scientific, and slightly dramatic argument for why [Peeps are amazing / Peeps are disgusting]. Include fake citations. Make it sound like a Supreme Court ruling.
```

### 6. Easter Movie Marathon

```text
Build me the perfect Easter movie marathon schedule from morning to night. Mix family-friendly, classic, and underrated picks. Include snack pairings for each movie.
```

### 7. Thank You Note Writer

```text
Write a heartfelt but not cheesy thank you message to [person] for [hosting Easter/the gift/making dinner]. Keep it genuine, warm, and under 4 sentences.
```
