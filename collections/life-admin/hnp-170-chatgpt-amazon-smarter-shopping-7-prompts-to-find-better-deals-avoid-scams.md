# ChatGPT + Amazon = smarter shopping: 7 prompts to find better deals, avoid scams, and save time

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Life Admin** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/chatgpt-amazon-smarter-shopping-copy-these-7-prompts-to-find-better-deals-avoid-/10173652277210347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Product Research Prompt

```text
Act as a product research assistant. I'm looking for a [insert item]. Give me a comparison of the top-rated options under [$XX] with pros, cons, and best-use cases.
```

### 2. Price Tracker Prompt

```text
How can I track the price history of this Amazon product: [insert product link]? Recommend tools or strategies to know if I'm getting the best deal.
```

### 3. Fake Review Filter Prompt

```text
Teach me how to spot fake or manipulated Amazon reviews. What red flags or patterns should I watch for before trusting the rating?
```

### 4. Alternative Finder Prompt

```text
Find me a similar product to [insert product] that's better quality, more budget-friendly, or comes with better reviews or warranty.
```

### 5. Time-Saving Search Prompt

```text
Based on this product type ([insert type]), write an optimized Amazon search string to help me find top-rated, best-selling products with fast delivery.
```

### 6. Ethical Purchase Prompt

```text
Suggest top-rated eco-friendly or ethically made alternatives to [insert product]. Prioritize sustainable packaging and verified ethical sourcing.
```

### 7. Bundle & Save Prompt

```text
I'm buying [insert list of products]. Suggest Amazon bundles or sellers offering discounts for purchasing items together.
```
