# ⚡ 5 fill-in-the-blank ChatGPT templates for real life

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Life Admin** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPTPromptGenius/comments/1uvbo1m/5_fillintheblank_chatgpt_templates_for_real_life/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Fridge-to-meals

```text
Using ingredients [INGREDIENTS] and constraints [DIET_TIME_SKILL_SERVINGS], suggest three realistic meals ordered by least extra shopping; list extras, time, and simple steps.
```

### 2. Realistic trip planner

```text
Plan [DESTINATION_AND_DATES] for [TRAVELERS] at [BUDGET_AND_PACE]. Leave breathing room, prioritize two or three highlights, and note transport, base area, and facts to verify.
```

### 3. Gift finder

```text
Suggest five gifts for [PERSON_DESCRIPTION] for [OCCASION] within [BUDGET], avoiding [EXCLUSIONS]. Explain the personal fit and include one safe and one unexpected option.
```

### 4. Buying decision

```text
Compare [OPTIONS] for my use case [USE_CASE] on [CRITERIA]. Recommend one, explain who should choose differently, and list current specs or prices I must verify.
```

### 5. Five-minute reset

```text
Break [AVOIDED_TASK] into the smallest five-minute start, a realistic sequence for [AVAILABLE_TIME], and one maintenance rule that prevents the backlog returning.
```
