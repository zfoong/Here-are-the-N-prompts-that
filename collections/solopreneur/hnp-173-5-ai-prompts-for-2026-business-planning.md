# 5 AI prompts for 2026 business planning

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Solopreneur** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/5-ai-prompts-for-2026-business-planning/25951254574480698/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. The 2026 Strategic Master Plan (The "All-In-One")

```text
Act as a Fortune 500 Chief Strategy Officer and Operations Expert.

Here is the context: I run a [Insert Business Type, e.g., Boutique Marketing Agency] with a team of [Insert Team Size]. Our primary goal for 2026 is to reach [Insert Revenue Goal, e.g., $1M ARR]. Our biggest current bottleneck is [Insert Bottleneck, e.g., lead generation or manual client onboarding].

Please write a comprehensive 2026 Strategic Business Plan. Include the following sections:

Executive Summary of the strategy.

A Month-by-Month Breakdown (Jan-Dec 2026) with one primary focus area per month.

A "Tech & Automation" section suggesting 3 specific tools or workflows to fix our bottleneck.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) we should track weekly.

Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 2. The Automation Ecosystem Architect

```text
Act as a CTO and Workflow Automation Specialist (Zapier/Make Expert).

Here is the context: For 2026, I want to reduce my manual workload by 40%. Currently, I spend too much time manually [Insert Repetitive Task 1] and [Insert Repetitive Task 2]. My current software stack includes [Insert Tools, e.g., Gmail, Trello, Slack].

Please design a "2026 Automation Workflow." Map out 3 specific "Zaps" or automations I should build to connect these tools. Explain exactly what the trigger and action should be for each to save me the most time.

Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 3. The 2026 Content & Authority Calendar

```text
Act as a Chief Marketing Officer (CMO).

Here is the context: My business targets [Insert Ideal Customer Profile] and we primarily use [Insert Channels, e.g., LinkedIn and Newsletter] for marketing. In 2026, I want to be seen as a thought leader in [Insert Niche Topic].

Please generate a "Q1 2026 Content Strategy" (Jan-March).

Assign a "Content Theme" for each month.

Provide 4 high-level headline ideas for each month.

Suggest one "Hero Asset" (like a webinar or ebook) I should launch in Q1 to generate leads.

Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 4. The "Cash Flow Confidence" Forecaster

```text
Act as a CFO and Financial Planner.

Here is the context: I am planning my 2026 budget. My goal is to hit [Insert Revenue Goal] with a profit margin of [Insert Desired Margin %]. My fixed monthly costs are roughly [Insert Fixed Cost Amount].

Please create a simplified "2026 Financial Scenario."

Break down how many units/clients I need to sell per month to hit the goal.

Flag potential cash-flow danger zones (e.g., slow summer months).

Suggest a budget allocation for "R&D/Experimentation" based on my revenue.

Ask me any questions you have.
```

### 5. The Quarterly "Sprint & Review" Protocol

```text
Act as an Agile Project Manager and Business Coach.

Here is the context: I often start the year strong but lose focus by March. I need a system to keep my 2026 plan on track.

Please design a "Quarterly Review Protocol" for me.

List the 5 hard questions I must ask myself and my team at the end of every quarter.

Create a standardized agenda for a "Quarterly Strategy Day" (4-hour duration) to reset our goals for the next sprint.

Ask me any questions you have.
```
