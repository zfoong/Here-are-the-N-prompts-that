# I compiled 500+ ChatGPT prompts for business owners — here are 10 free ones

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Solopreneur** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPTPromptGenius/comments/1qyel1j/i_compiled_500_chatgpt_prompts_for_business)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Business prompt 1

```text
Write 5 email subject lines for [PRODUCT] that create urgency without being clickbait
```

### 2. Business prompt 2

```text
Create a 30-day content calendar for [BUSINESS] targeting [AUDIENCE] on [PLATFORM]
```

### 3. Business prompt 3

```text
Act as a management consultant. Analyze my business model: [DESCRIBE]. Identify the 3 biggest risks and suggest mitigation strategies
```

### 4. Business prompt 4

```text
I'm entering [MARKET]. List 10 questions I should answer before launching
```

### 5. Business prompt 5

```text
Write a LinkedIn post about [TOPIC] that hooks in the first line, delivers value, and ends with a question
```

### 6. Business prompt 6

```text
Turn these bullet points into a compelling story: [BULLETS]
```

### 7. Business prompt 7

```text
I have these tasks today: [LIST]. Prioritize them using the Eisenhower Matrix and explain why
```

### 8. Business prompt 8

```text
Create a weekly review template I can use every Friday to reflect on wins, losses, and next week's priorities
```

### 9. Business prompt 9

```text
Write a cold email to [ROLE] at [COMPANY TYPE] offering [SERVICE]. Keep it under 100 words
```

### 10. Business prompt 10

```text
List 10 objections a [CUSTOMER TYPE] might have about [PRODUCT] and write responses for each
```
