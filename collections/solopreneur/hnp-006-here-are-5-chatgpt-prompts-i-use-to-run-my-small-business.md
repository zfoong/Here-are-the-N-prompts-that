# 💼 Here are 5 ChatGPT prompts I use to run my small business

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Solopreneur** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/HowToEntrepreneur/comments/1sdz87s/here_are_5_chatgpt_prompts_i_use_to_run_my_small/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Overdue invoice

```text
Write a firm, courteous payment reminder for invoice [NUMBER], amount [AMOUNT], due [DATE], and follow-up stage [STAGE]. State a new deadline and one next step without threats.
```

### 2. Negative review response

```text
Draft a public reply to this review [REVIEW]. Acknowledge the specific issue, avoid admitting unverified facts, offer a practical resolution path, and move private details off-platform.
```

### 3. Job posting

```text
Create a job post for [ROLE] using [RESPONSIBILITIES], [MUST_HAVE_SKILLS], [PAY_RANGE], and [LOCATION]. Separate essentials from preferences and use inclusive, concrete language.
```

### 4. Instagram captions

```text
Generate [COUNT] caption options for [BUSINESS] about [TOPIC], each with a distinct angle, natural voice, accurate details, and a relevant low-pressure call to action.
```

### 5. Difficult customer reply

```text
Draft a calm response to [CUSTOMER_MESSAGE]. Summarize the concern, state what can and cannot be done under [POLICY], and offer the clearest next step.
```
