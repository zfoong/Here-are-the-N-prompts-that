# I was shocked to discover these 5 ChatGPT prompts that helped business owners scale in just a month

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Solopreneur** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/i-was-shocked-to-discover-these-5-chatgpt-prompts-that-helped-business-owners-to/10173153045840347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Sales funnel builder

```text
Create a detailed sales funnel for [specific product/service] that includes lead magnets, email sequences, and upsell strategies.
```

### 2. 30-day social media plan

```text
Design a 30-day social media content plan that focuses on engaging my audience and driving traffic to [specific offer].
```

### 3. Audience personas

```text
Analyze my target audience for [specific product/service] and provide detailed personas, including pain points, desires, and purchasing behavior.
```

### 4. Pricing optimization

```text
Review my current pricing strategy and suggest ways to optimize it for higher revenue without alienating customers.
```

### 5. Strategic partnerships

```text
Suggest innovative ways to form strategic partnerships or collaborations that could expand my business reach.
```
