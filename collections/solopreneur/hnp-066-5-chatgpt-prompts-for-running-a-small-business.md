# Here are 5 ChatGPT prompts I use to run my small business faster

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Solopreneur** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/AIToolsAndTips/comments/1sdz6sl/here_are_5_chatgpt_prompts_i_use_to_run_my_small)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Overdue invoice follow-up

```text
Write a professional follow-up email for a payment that is 14 days overdue. Polite but firm. Include [invoice number] and [amount] as placeholders. Offer to resend the invoice.
```

### 2. Negative Google review

```text
Write a response to this negative Google review: [paste review here]. Be professional, empathetic, offer a resolution, keep it under 100 words.
```

### 3. Job posting

```text
Write a job posting for a [job title] at my [business type]. Include key responsibilities, requirements, what we offer, and how to apply. Professional but approachable tone.
```

### 4. Instagram captions

```text
Write 5 Instagram captions for a [business type] promoting [product]. Conversational tone, under 150 words each, include a call to action.
```

### 5. Difficult customer

```text
Write a calm professional email response to a customer threatening a bad review unless we give them something free. Don't give in but try to resolve the issue.
```
