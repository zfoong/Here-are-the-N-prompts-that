# 7 AI prompts for turning ideas into extra income

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Money Making** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/jonathanjmast/posts/7-ai-prompts-for-turning-ideas-into-extra-income/24925505053722327/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Idea Clarity Map

```text
Act as an idea mapping coach. Here is my list of ideas: [INSERT IDEAS]. Help me group them into categories (digital products, services, physical goods, partnerships, etc.) so I can see patterns and pick one to focus on first.
```

### 2. Feasibility Checker

```text
Act as a startup strategist. Take my idea: [INSERT IDEA]. Evaluate it for: (1) ease of starting, (2) cost to launch, (3) time commitment, and (4) income potential. Then rate it low/medium/high for each factor.
```

### 3. Low-Cost Test Plan

```text
Act as a lean startup mentor. My idea is [INSERT IDEA]. Design a plan to test if people will pay for it with less than $100 and within 2 weeks. Suggest how to build a 'minimum viable version' (MVP).
```

### 4. Skill & Resource Matchmaker

```text
Act as a resource optimization coach. Here are my skills, tools, and resources: [INSERT LIST]. From my idea list [INSERT IDEAS], recommend which ideas best align with what I already have.
```

### 5. Income Pathway Planner

```text
Act as a monetization strategist. My idea is [INSERT IDEA]. Suggest 3 different ways I could make money from it (subscription, one-time sales, services, affiliate, ads, etc.).
```

### 6. Time-to-Cash Roadmap

```text
Act as a business launch coach. Take my idea: [INSERT IDEA]. Create a step-by-step action plan for the next 30 days to go from idea to first income.
```

### 7. Focus & Accountability Guide

```text
Act as a productivity accountability partner. I have multiple ideas [INSERT IDEAS]. Help me pick ONE to focus on first and design a simple system to keep me committed for 30 days.
```
