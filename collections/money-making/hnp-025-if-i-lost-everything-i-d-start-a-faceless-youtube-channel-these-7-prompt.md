# ✍️ If I lost everything, I'd start a faceless YouTube channel. These 7 prompts provide the blueprint.

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Money Making** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/threalmelaninking/posts/if-i-lost-everything-id-start-a-faceless-youtube-channel-right-awayno-face-no-mi/754579557427080/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Niche shortlist

```text
Using current cited data, propose 12 faceless YouTube niches for [SKILLS_AND_MARKET]. Score demand, competition, monetization diversity, research burden, and policy risk; avoid invented CPM figures.
```

### 2. Audience-question research

```text
Find 18 current questions people ask about [NICHE] from accessible search and community evidence. Cite each source and cluster by intent.
```

### 3. Short video script

```text
Write a 65-second script on [TOPIC] from [SOURCE_NOTES] with hook, concise explanation, example, and CTA. Flag any visual or factual claim needing verification.
```

### 4. Title and thumbnail concepts

```text
Create ten accurate title-and-thumbnail pairs for [VIDEO], each with one clear promise and no misleading imagery or result claim.
```

### 5. Commercial-safe asset research

```text
List six potential sources for visuals, B-roll, or screen recordings and summarize their current licenses; require me to verify each asset's specific terms before use.
```

### 6. Monetization map

```text
Compare four monetization options for a [NICHE] channel without ad revenue, including audience fit, prerequisites, disclosure duties, and validation steps.
```

### 7. Twenty-eight-day schedule

```text
Create a 28-day publishing experiment for [NICHE] and [CAPACITY], with topics, format, production budget, success metrics, and weekly adjustment rules.
```
