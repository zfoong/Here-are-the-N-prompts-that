# 💼 I changed how I ask ChatGPT for side hustle ideas. Here are 7 prompts that actually work.

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Money Making** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPT/comments/1q09pec/i_changed_how_i_ask_chatgpt_for_side_hustle_ideas/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Side-hustle discovery

```text
Given my skills [SKILLS], weekly time [HOURS], budget [BUDGET], location [MARKET], and dislikes [AVOID], propose ten side hustles and score each for fit, startup friction, and first-sale speed.
```

### 2. Market validation

```text
Design a seven-day validation plan for [IDEA] aimed at [CUSTOMER]. Specify who to interview, what to ask, a no-build test, and numeric stop/continue thresholds.
```

### 3. Hidden niche finder

```text
Break [BROAD_MARKET] into underserved micro-niches. For each, name the recurring problem, current workaround, reachable community, and evidence I should gather before acting.
```

### 4. One-page business plan

```text
Create a one-page plan for [OFFER]: customer, problem, promise, channel, price hypothesis, costs, risks, first experiment, and the next seven days.
```

### 5. First ten customers

```text
Plan how to find the first ten customers for [OFFER] without paid ads. Give daily outreach actions, a short message template, tracking fields, and ethical follow-up limits.
```

### 6. Marketing copy

```text
Using [CUSTOMER_LANGUAGE] and [OFFER_DETAILS], draft a headline, three benefit bullets, proof placeholders, objection answers, and one low-pressure call to action.
```

### 7. Problem solver

```text
For this stalled side hustle [CONTEXT], identify the likely bottleneck, questions needed to confirm it, three experiments, and the cheapest next action.
```
