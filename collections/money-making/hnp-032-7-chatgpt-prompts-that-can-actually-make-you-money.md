# 7 ChatGPT prompts that can actually make you money

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Money Making** · [Original post ↗](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPThadSaid/comments/1nabzp1/chatgpthadsaid_7_chatgpt_prompts_that_can/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Freelance Blog Writer

```text
Write a 1,000-word blog post on [topic] with SEO headers, keyword-rich intro, and a clear call to action.
```

### 2. Product Research Assistant

```text
Summarize the top 10 complaints in Amazon reviews for [product] and suggest improvements.
```

### 3. Ebook Creator

```text
Outline a step-by-step beginner’s guide to [niche]. Break it into 8 chapters with bullet points.
```

### 4. Email Copywriter

```text
Write a 5-part email sequence to sell [product/service] with curiosity-driven subject lines.
```

### 5. Social Media Ghostwriter

```text
Write 10 viral Twitter posts about [topic] with a mix of humor, tips, and curiosity hooks.
```

### 6. Ad Copy Generator

```text
Write 5 Facebook ad variations for [product] with different tones (curious, urgent, funny, testimonial).
```

### 7. Course Builder

```text
Create a lesson plan for a beginner course on [skill]. Include module titles, key learning points, and assignments.
```
