# 🧰 The $1,240 flight I booked for $620: 7 ChatGPT prompts airlines don't want you to see

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Field Guides** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/maryhendersoncoaching/videos/the-1240-flight-i-booked-for-620-7-chatgpt-prompts-airlines-dont-want-you-to-see/1838410977103897/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Cheapest route finder

```text
Find plausible routes from [ORIGIN] to [DESTINATION] during [DATE_RANGE], including nearby airports and separate-leg options. Use current cited fares and include all-in costs and connection risks.
```

### 2. Budget-airline finder

```text
List carriers serving [ROUTE] that may be missing from major aggregators. Cite current schedules and send me to official airline sites to verify.
```

### 3. Stopover comparison

```text
Compare up to three stopover cities for [ROUTE] on total price, elapsed time, baggage transfer, visa rules, and missed-connection protection using current sources.
```

### 4. Sale and fare check

```text
Search current official airline sales and reputable fare sources for [ROUTE_AND_DATES]. Report timestamped evidence and say when no verified deal is found.
```

### 5. Cross-site comparison

```text
Build a same-itinerary comparison across [SITES] including taxes, baggage, seats, payment fees, cancellation rules, and booking-party risk.
```

### 6. Price-alert setup

```text
Explain how to configure legitimate price alerts for [ROUTE] below [THRESHOLD] using [SUPPORTED_SERVICES]; do not claim to monitor or notify unless a real integration exists.
```

### 7. Round-trip versus one-way

```text
Compare a round trip with mixed one-way tickets for [ROUTE], including total cost, baggage, change rules, and loss of through-ticket protection.
```
