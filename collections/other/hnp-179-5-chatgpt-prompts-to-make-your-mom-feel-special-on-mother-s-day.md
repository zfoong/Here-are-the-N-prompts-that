# 5 ChatGPT prompts to make your mom feel special on Mother's Day

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Field Guides** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/Owwstin/posts/today-is-mothers-dayuse-these-5-chatgpt-prompts-to-make-your-mom-feel-special-to/10162083845791256/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Heartfelt Personalized Letter

```text
Write a heartfelt Mother's Day letter to my mom. Her name is [NAME]. Some things I love about her: [list 3-5 things]. A favorite memory I have with her is [memory]. Make it emotional, personal, and around 250 words. Tone: warm, sincere, not cheesy.
```

### 2. Custom Photo Card

```text
Using the uploaded photo, create a personalized Mother's Day card. Frame it with soft watercolor florals (pink roses + eucalyptus). Add elegant serif text at the top reading 'Happy Mother's Day' and beneath it 'With all my love.' Background: warm cream. Color palette: blush pink, gold, sage green. Print-ready, vertical format, high resolution.
```

### 3. Restored Old Photo

```text
Restore this old family photograph with maximum realism. Repair tears, scratches, faded corners, and discoloration. Preserve the original expressions and faces exactly. Recover detail in hair, clothing, and background. Apply soft warm tones natural to the era. Output: high resolution, nostalgic but crisp.
```

### 4. Perfect Gift Brainstorm

```text
Help me find the perfect Mother's Day gift. About my mom: age [X], her hobbies are [list], she loves [interests], and recently she's been into [recent interest]. My budget is $[X]. Give me 10 thoughtful, specific gift ideas (not generic stuff like 'flowers'). Include why each one fits her.
```

### 5. Mom Tribute Instagram Post

```text
Write me 3 Instagram caption options for a Mother's Day post about my mom. Her name is [NAME]. What makes her special: [list 2-3 things]. Tone options: 1) Funny + sweet, 2) Deeply emotional, 3) Short and punchy. Include 5 relevant hashtags for each.
```
