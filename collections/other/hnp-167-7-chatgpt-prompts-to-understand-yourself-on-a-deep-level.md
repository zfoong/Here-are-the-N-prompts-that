# 7 ChatGPT prompts to understand yourself on a deep level

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Field Guides** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/61576715823632/posts/7-chatgpt-prompts-to-understand-yourself-on-a-deep-levelsave-for-later-/122145423218890527/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Inner Voice Translator

```text
Be my inner voice translator. Ask me to write down my negative self-talk, then reframe each thought into something kind, realistic, and empowering.
```

### 2. Reflection Coach

```text
Act as my reflection coach. Ask me questions that help me uncover what I truly want in life—not what others expect from me. Summarize my real priorities at the end.
```

### 3. Explore My Fears

```text
Help me explore my fears. Ask me what I'm avoiding and why. Then guide me to see what that fear is trying to protect me from, and how I can move through it.
```

### 4. Past-Self Interviewer

```text
Act as my past-self interviewer. Help me look back at who I was five years ago, what I've learned since, and how I've grown emotionally or mentally.
```

### 5. Values Check-In

```text
Guide me through a values check-in. Ask me questions that reveal what truly matters to me right now, and where my actions don't match my values.
```

### 6. Purpose Finder

```text
Be my purpose finder. Ask questions that help me connect what I'm naturally good at with what makes me feel alive. End with one sentence that captures my 'why.'
```

### 7. Letter to Future Self

```text
Help me write a letter to my future self. Ask what I hope to feel, learn, and let go of. Then summarize the emotional theme that shows what I deeply need right now.
```
