# How to use ChatGPT to book a hotel: 8 high-impact prompts

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Field Guides** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/iamIzuken/posts/how-to-use-chatgpt-to-book-hotelhere-are-8-most-powerful-high-impact-prompts-for/10092639564148273/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Local Rate Unlocker

```text
Search for hotel rates in [City] that are cheaper when booked from a local IP or in local currency. Compare prices with and without VPN. Highlight sites like Agoda, Traveloka, or local booking engines with region-based pricing.
```

### 2. Price Drop Radar

```text
Check hotel price trends for [City] in [Month]. Which 3-day ranges show the biggest drops? Act like Google Hotels or Hopper and suggest the exact dates to book a 4-star stay for the lowest price.
```

### 3. Free Night Stack Hack

```text
Search loyalty programs or credit card offers that stack free hotel nights when you book 3+ nights in [City]. Include which brands (like Marriott, Hilton, Accor) offer 4th or 5th night free, and how to qualify with minimal points or fees.
```

### 4. Near but Cheaper

```text
Find hotels near [Popular Area or Landmark] that are 30–60% cheaper but offer similar quality or better amenities. Show walking distance or metro access, and mention if they're quieter or safer.
```

### 5. AI-Powered Hotel Matchmaker

```text
I want a hotel in [City] that matches this vibe: chill rooftop, close to cafes, 24/7 check-in, under $40/night, and good for solo travelers. Act like an AI hotel stylist and give me 3 options with reasons
```

### 6. Travel Creator Hotel Collab

```text
Write a short, engaging pitch I can send to boutique hotels in [City] offering to exchange social content or shoutouts for a discounted or free stay. Make me sound professional, creative, and trustworthy.
```

### 7. DM for Discount

```text
Find boutique hotels or guesthouses in [City] that are active on Instagram. I want to DM them to request a direct booking discount. Write a short, friendly, professional message I can copy-paste — mention content creation, referrals, or repeat stays.
```

### 8. Hotel Price Forecast

```text
Scan hotel rates in [City] for [Month] or [Range of Dates]. Identify the cheapest 2–3 date windows for stays and explain if prices will rise or drop soon based on demand and season. Use Hopper-style price forecasting.
```
