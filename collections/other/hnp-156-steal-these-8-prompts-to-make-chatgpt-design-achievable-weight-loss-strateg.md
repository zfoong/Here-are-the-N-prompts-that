# Steal these 8 prompts to make ChatGPT design achievable weight-loss strategies

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Field Guides** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/stormyguld/posts/steal-these-8-prompts-to-make-chatgpt-design-achievable-weight-loss-strategies/828990296608968/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. Personalized Weekly Plan

```text
Create a simple, realistic 7-day weight-loss plan for me based on my lifestyle. Include meals, snacks, hydration goals, and short daily workouts. Keep the plan easy to follow and explain why each element helps with weight loss.
```

### 2. Behavior-Based Strategy

```text
Design a weight-loss strategy focused on small habit changes rather than intense dieting. Suggest 8–10 micro-habits I can start this week and show how each contributes to sustainable fat loss.
```

### 3. Time-Limited Eating

```text
Give me an achievable weight-loss strategy using time-restricted eating. I want a simple daily schedule, meal ideas, and guidance on how to ease into it without feeling hungry or fatigued.
```

### 4. Desk-Worker Strategy

```text
Create a weight-loss plan for someone who works at a desk most of the day. Include practical movement breaks, calorie-smart meal ideas, and weekend activity goals.
```

### 5. No-Gym Workout Plan

```text
Develop an effective weight-loss strategy that doesn't require a gym. Include bodyweight workouts, walking routines, motivation tips, and a weekly structure that gradually increases difficulty.
```

### 6. Emotional-Eating Control

```text
Design a weight-loss strategy that focuses on managing emotional eating. Include triggers to watch for, alternative behaviors, simple meal guidelines, and a weekly check-in routine.
```

### 7. Beginner Nutrition Blueprint

```text
Give me a beginner-friendly nutrition plan for steady weight loss. Include portion guidance, grocery list essentials, high-protein/low-effort meals, and daily calorie-saving swaps.
```

### 8. Weight-Loss + Strength Plan

```text
Create a weight-loss plan that emphasizes building strength while reducing body fat. Include a balanced weekly workout routine, protein goals, recovery tips, and sample meals.
```
