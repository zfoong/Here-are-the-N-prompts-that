# 🧰 10 ChatGPT prompt templates that always work

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Field Guides** · [Original post ↗](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/learn-leadership-net_10-chatgpt-prompt-templates-that-always-work-activity-7379877906543468544-plMt)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace every `[BRACKETED_VARIABLE]` with your own context.

### 1. Expert role

```text
Act as a [RELEVANT_EXPERT] and help with [TASK] using [CONTEXT]. State assumptions, limits, and the evidence behind recommendations.
```

### 2. Step-by-step guide

```text
Explain how to complete [TASK] in [COUNT] steps for [SKILL_LEVEL], including prerequisites, checkpoints, common errors, and completion criteria.
```

### 3. Compare options

```text
Compare [OPTIONS] against [CRITERIA], costs, long-term trade-offs, and uncertainty; recommend by user type rather than declaring one universal winner.
```

### 4. Idea generator

```text
Generate [COUNT] ideas for [GOAL] aimed at [AUDIENCE]. Give each a distinct angle, rationale, required evidence, and first test.
```

### 5. Rewrite text

```text
Rewrite [TEXT] for [AUDIENCE] in [TONE] while preserving facts and intent. Flag any claim that cannot be retained safely.
```

### 6. Review and improve

```text
Review [DRAFT] against [CRITERIA]. Separate correctness, clarity, evidence, and style; rank improvements by importance.
```

### 7. Scenario simulation

```text
Simulate [SCENARIO] one turn at a time at [DIFFICULTY]. Stay in role, adapt to my answers, then provide a rubric-based debrief.
```

### 8. Format request

```text
Convert [CONTENT] into [FORMAT] for [CHANNEL]. Follow [STRUCTURAL_CONSTRAINTS] and preserve attribution, facts, and caveats.
```

### 9. Essentials checklist

```text
Create a prioritized checklist for [GOAL] with owner, prerequisite, completion condition, and common omission for each item.
```

### 10. Structured brainstorm

```text
Brainstorm options for [PROBLEM], then cluster them, surface trade-offs and failure modes, and select three ideas worth testing.
```
