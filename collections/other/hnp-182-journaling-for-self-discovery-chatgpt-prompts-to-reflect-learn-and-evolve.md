# Journaling for self-discovery: ChatGPT prompts to reflect, learn, and evolve

[← All prompt packs](../../CATALOG.md) · **Field Guides** · [Original post ↗](https://www.facebook.com/adrian.anthony.brambila/posts/journaling-for-self-discoveryuse-these-chatgpt-prompts-to-reflect-learn-and-evol/10173784049950347/)

## Prompts

Copy a prompt and replace the bracketed text with your own context.

### 1. "Who Am I, Really?" Prompt

```text
Ask me 5 deep questions to help me understand who I truly am beyond roles, achievements, or external expectations.
```

### 2. Growth Reflection Prompt

```text
Help me reflect on how I've grown over the past 6 months — emotionally, mentally, or spiritually. Ask follow-up questions too.
```

### 3. Inner Critic Check-In Prompt

```text
Guide me through a journal entry that helps me identify and gently challenge my most common self-limiting beliefs.
```

### 4. Emotional Clarity Prompt

```text
Today, I feel [insert feeling]. Help me unpack why I might be feeling this way, what it's connected to, and what I need most right now.
```

### 5. Vision Alignment Prompt

```text
Lead me through a visualization journal exercise that helps me align my daily habits with the future I want to create.
```
